# atlas

Story Atlas, the private planning side of every story (epic S1-E17, `docs/stories/s1-e17-story-atlas.md`; bundle ATLAS). It complements the writer section in `src/features/writing/`: the same stories, the same chapters, with the plan beside them. Started by ATLAS-a (S1-E17-01 to S1-E17-06).

- `components/AtlasHomePage.tsx`: `/plan`; the list of story plans (`StoryPlanCard`, `useMyStoryPlans` → `my_story_plans()`), the one planned last on this device first, and "Plan a new story" (G10a).
- `components/StoryAtlasPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug`; `AtlasTopBar` (`StorySwitcher`, Write, New story) and `AtlasPageHeading` and `AtAGlance`: `StorySummary`, `WordsProgress`, `ChaptersAtAGlance`, `StoryDetailsSection`, `LinkedProjectSection`, and a side column with `CastAtAGlance`, `RecentChaptersList`, `CharacterFieldsSection` and the writer section's `DeleteStoryControl` (G10a).
- Fields that save by themselves: `PlanTextField`, `PlanSelectField`, `WordTargetField`, with `SaveNote`; the logic is `hooks/useAutosaveField.ts`.
- `hooks/useStoryPlan.ts` and `api/storyPlans.ts`: `story_plan()`, `update_story_plan()` and, for the title, genre and audience, `update_my_story()`.
- `lib/`: `lastPlannedStory.ts` (the device's last story), `planProgress.ts` (the words bar), `wordTarget.ts` (reading a typed target), `linkedProject.ts` (http and https only).
- `components/CharactersPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/characters/:characterId?` (ATLAS-b, S1-E17-08 to 13): `CastToolbar`, `CastCards` (no character open), `CastList` (beside an open sheet), `CharacterSheet` with `CharacterSheetHeader` and its sections, `CharacterAvatar` (a picture or nothing; G10a); `AtlasSectionNav` under the top bar. On the at-a-glance page, `CastAtAGlance` and `CharacterFieldsSection`.
- `hooks/useDetailChangers.ts` and `api/characterDetails.ts`: where a writer's own details sit on the sheet (under a built-in group or in a group of their own) and renaming a group (GAPS-1); `lib/ownDetailGroups.ts`.
- `hooks/useStoryCast.ts` and `api/characters.ts`: `story_cast()` and the character, field and picture functions; `lib/castGroups.ts`, `lib/characterInitials.ts`, `lib/writeAddress.ts`.
- `components/ConnectionsPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/connections` (ATLAS-c, S1-E17-14 to 16): `AddRelationshipForm`, `RelationshipMap`, `FamilyTreeView` and `RelationshipList` (also on each sheet through `CharacterRelationshipsSection`). `hooks/useRelationshipChangers.ts` adds and deletes; `hooks/useRelationshipWords.ts` writes a relationship as a sentence or a line label; `lib/relationshipCategories.ts` (each category's colour and line style), `lib/relationshipMap.ts` (the map's layout), `lib/familyTree.ts` and `lib/familyTreeLayout.ts` (the tree's generations and drawing).
- The relationship form (G10b): `AddRelationshipForm` with `RelationshipKindPickers`, `CousinPickers`, `RelationshipOwnWords` and `RelationshipClashPanel`; the pure helpers `lib/relationshipGroups.ts`, `lib/cousinWords.ts` and `lib/relationshipClash.ts`.
- The map and tree (G10b2): `ConnectionsMapView`, `RelationshipMap` with `MapCards`, `MapLines`, `MapCategorySwitches` and `MapZoomBar`; the hooks `useCardDrag` and `useMapZoom`; `FamilyTreeView` with Whose tree and the line legend.
- Story world and Storyline in glass (G10c1): `BibleContents`, `BibleEntryView`, `BoardCard`, `BoardColumnSection`, `TimelineRow` (shared by `TimelineView` and `TimelineEventItem`) and `PovLanesView` with its teller tiles.
- The Narrator as a chapter's point of view (G10c2): `lib/pointOfView.ts`, `lib/povLanes.ts`, the option in `ChapterPlanBasics` and the Narrator lane in `PovLanesView`.
- The Marketing page's QR code (G10c3): `components/StoryQrCard.tsx`, `lib/storyQr.ts` and `lib/downloadQr.ts`; drawn in the browser, nothing stored.
- `components/BiblePage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/world/:entryId?`, shown as "Story world" (ATLAS-d, S1-E17-17 and 18; renamed from the story bible on 2 Nov 2026, ADMIN-2a2, while the code keeps "bible"; `OldBibleAddressRedirect` keeps old `/bible` links working): `BibleContents`, `BibleEntryView` (reading with `BibleMarkdown`, editing with `BibleEntryEditor`) and `BibleSectionsManager` with `BibleSectionRow`. `hooks/useStoryBible.ts` reads and changes it through `api/bible.ts`; `lib/bibleTags.ts` reads and writes the tags box; `TwoStepDelete` is the shared delete that asks twice.
- The writing view (ATLAS-e, S1-E17-23 to 26), shown inside the chapter editor: `components/WritingChapterList.tsx`, `ChapterPlanPanel.tsx` (with `ChapterPlanBasics`, `ChapterPlanSetting`, `PresentCharacterChips`, `ChapterScenesSection` and `ChapterSceneRow`), `ChapterWordsLine.tsx` and `FocusModeToggle.tsx`; `CharacterAppearancesSection.tsx` on each sheet (S1-E17-12). `api/chapterPlans.ts` with `hooks/useChapterPlan.ts`, `useChapterProgress.ts` and `useCharacterAppearances.ts`; `lib/optionalNumbers.ts` (the target and sort boxes) and `lib/chapterLength.ts` (the long-chapter warning). `api/checkedAnswer.ts` is the shared check on every change's answer.
- `components/StorylinePage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/storyline` (ATLAS-f, S1-E17-19 to 22): `ChapterBoard` (with `BoardColumnSection`, `BoardCard`, `BoardCardMoves` and `BoardMoveDialog`), `TimelineView` (with `AddEventForm` and `TimelineEventItem`) and `PovLanesView`; `PlanningStatusPill` shows a status in colour and words. `api/storyline.ts` with `hooks/useStoryline.ts`, `useBoardMoves.ts` and `useChapterLabels.ts`; `lib/boardColumns.ts`, `lib/timeline.ts`, `lib/povLanes.ts` and `lib/storylineView.ts`.
- Acts on the board (GAPS-2a): `components/AddActForm.tsx` ("Add act") and `components/EditActForm.tsx` ("Edit act": rename, or delete after a question; the chapters move to "No act"), through `useStoryline`.
- Marketing details of the writer's own (GAPS-2b): `components/MarketingDetails.tsx` ("Add detail") and `components/MarketingDetailItem.tsx` (the box, "Edit detail": rename, or delete after a question), through `useStoryMarketing`; the back cover's byline is in `BackCoverPreview`.
- `components/IdeasPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/ideas` (ATLAS-g, S1-E17-27 and 28; glass rows in G10c3): `IdeaQuickAdd` and `IdeaItem`, with `api/ideas.ts`, `hooks/useStoryIdeas.ts` and `lib/ideaFilters.ts`. `components/MarketingPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/marketing` (S1-E17-29): `MarketingFields` and `BackCoverPreview`, with `api/marketing.ts`, `hooks/useStoryMarketing.ts` and `hooks/useStoryChoiceOptions.ts`.
- `components/AtlasSearchPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/search` (ATLAS-h, S1-E17-30), fed by `AtlasSearchBox` in the top bar: `SearchResultItem` and `HighlightedSnippet`, with `api/search.ts`, `hooks/useStorySearch.ts`, `lib/searchGroups.ts`, `lib/highlightMatches.ts` and `lib/itemAddress.ts`. `[[Name]]` cross-links (S1-E17-31): `lib/crossLinks.ts`, `api/links.ts`, `hooks/useLinkTargets.ts`, `hooks/useOpenStorySlug.ts`, `CrossLink`, `CrossLinkText` and `CrossLinkChips`; "Mentioned in" (S1-E17-12) is `CharacterMentionsList` with `hooks/useCharacterMentions.ts`. `hooks/useScrollToHash.ts` brings an item opened from a result into view.
- What readers can see (ATLAS-i, S1-E17-32 to S1-E17-35): the writer's side is `ReaderViewPage` (`/plan/s/:storySlug/readers`) with `PublicAreaSwitches`, `ItemVisibilityControls`, `SheetPartsControls`, `ReaderVisibilitySection` and `PlanPreviewPage`, through `api/readerView.ts`, `hooks/usePlanVisibility.ts` and `hooks/usePlanPreview.ts`. The readers' side is `PublicPlanPage` (`/s/:storySlug/atlas/…`) with `PublicPlanView`, `PublicPlanNav`, `PublicCast`, `PublicCharacter`, `PublicConnections`, `PublicWorld`, `PublicTimeline`, `PlanGuardPill` and `MaybeLink`, through `api/publicPlan.ts`, `hooks/usePublicPlan.ts`, `hooks/useCrossLinks.ts` and `lib/publicPlanAreas.ts`. Since G10c4 the Readers page also lists each character (`ReaderCharactersList`, `ReaderCharacterRow`, `lib/readerSummary.ts`), and readers' pages and the preview share `PublicPlanHero`.
- `registerAtlasTranslations.ts`: the `atlas` text, added when Story Atlas first renders.
- The database side: `supabase/README.md`, "Stories", "Story Atlas plans", "Story Atlas characters", "Story Atlas relationships", "Story Atlas bible" and "Story Atlas chapter planning", "Story Atlas storyline", "Story Atlas questions and ideas, and marketing" and "Story Atlas search and cross-links".
