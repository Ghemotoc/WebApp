# atlas

Story Atlas, the private planning side of every story (epic S1-E17, `docs/stories/s1-e17-story-atlas.md`; bundle ATLAS). It complements the writer section in `src/features/writing/`: the same stories, the same chapters, with the plan beside them. Started by ATLAS-a (S1-E17-01 to S1-E17-06).

- `components/AtlasHomePage.tsx`: `/plan`; Story Atlas, one row per story (`StoryPlanRow`, `useMyStoryPlans` → `my_story_plans()`), the newest edit first (board E20, user stories bundle 6, story 6.1).
- `components/StoryAtlasPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug`; `AtlasFrame` (CALM-9a: `AtlasTopBar` with the breadcrumb, `StorySwitcher`, Story page, Write, `AtlasNewCharacter`, Add a relationship (both fold to icons after 3 visits, `lib/addHintVisits.ts`) and the search box; `AtlasSectionNav` as a side menu or a sideways line) and `AtlasPageHeading` and `AtAGlance` (board E21): the hero `StorySummary` with `WordsProgress` and `ChaptersAtAGlance`, then flat sections (`AtlasFlatSection`): `StoryDetailsSection`, `LinkedProjectSection`, `CharacterFieldsSection` and the writer section's `DeleteStoryControl` on the left, `CastAtAGlance` (with `CharacterFace`) and `RecentChaptersList` on the right.
- Fields that save by themselves: `PlanTextField`, `PlanSelectField`, `WordTargetField`, with `SaveNote`; the logic is `hooks/useAutosaveField.ts`.
- `hooks/useStoryPlan.ts` and `api/storyPlans.ts`: `story_plan()`, `update_story_plan()` and, for the title, genre and audience, `update_my_story()`.
- `lib/`: `planRowFacts.ts` (the words and last fact of a /plan row), `planProgress.ts` (the words bar), `wordTarget.ts` (reading a typed target), `linkedProject.ts` (http and https only).
- `components/CharactersPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/characters/:characterId?` (ATLAS-b, S1-E17-08 to 13): `CastList` (the filter, the count and the rows, boards E22 and E23), `CharacterSheet` with `CharacterSheetHeader` and its folding sections in one column (story 6.4, board E23), `CharacterFace` (initials until a drawn picture passes; story 6.3), `CharacterAvatar` (a picture or nothing, still used by the public plan and the relationship map; G10a); `AtlasSectionNav` in `AtlasFrame`; `NewCharacterButton` is shared with the top bar. On the at-a-glance page, `CastAtAGlance` and `CharacterFieldsSection`.
- `hooks/useDetailChangers.ts` and `api/characterDetails.ts`: where a writer's own details sit on the sheet (under a built-in group or in a group of their own) and renaming a group (GAPS-1); `lib/ownDetailGroups.ts`.
- `hooks/useDetailFormSave.ts`: the saving state and messages of "Add detail", "Add a group of details" and "Rename group" (one save at a time; "that name is taken" in plain words; GAPS-1 break test).
- `hooks/useStoryCast.ts` and `api/characters.ts`: `story_cast()` and the character, field and picture functions; `lib/castGroups.ts`, `lib/characterInitials.ts`, `lib/writeAddress.ts`.
- `components/ConnectionsPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/connections` (ATLAS-c, S1-E17-14 to 16): `AddRelationshipForm`, `RelationshipMap`, `FamilyTreeView` and `RelationshipList` (also on each sheet through `CharacterRelationshipsSection`). `hooks/useRelationshipChangers.ts` adds and deletes; `hooks/useRelationshipWords.ts` writes a relationship as a sentence or a line label; `lib/relationshipCategories.ts` (each category's colour and line style), `lib/relationshipMap.ts` (the map's layout), `lib/familyTree.ts` and `lib/familyTreeLayout.ts` (the tree's generations and drawing).
- The relationship form (G10b): `AddRelationshipForm` (in `AddRelationshipDialog` over a sheet, story 6.5) with `RelationshipKindPicker`, `CousinPickers`, `RelationshipOwnWords` and `RelationshipClashPanel`; the pure helpers `lib/relationshipGroups.ts`, `lib/cousinWords.ts` and `lib/relationshipClash.ts`.
- The map and tree (G10b2; redrawn by user story 6.6, board E25): `ConnectionsMapView` (the map, the list under it and "Add a relationship"), `RelationshipMap` with `MapCards`, `MapLines` and `MapCategorySwitches`, placed by family and circle in `lib/relationshipMap.ts`; `FamilyTreeView` with Whose tree and the line legend.
- Story world and Storyline in glass (G10c1): `BibleContents`, `BibleEntryView`, `BoardCard`, `BoardColumnSection` and `PovLanesView` (its teller tiles became one counts line in story 6.11).
- The Narrator as a chapter's point of view (G10c2): `lib/pointOfView.ts`, `lib/povLanes.ts`, the option in `ChapterPlanBasics` and the Narrator lane in `PovLanesView`.
- The Marketing page's QR code (G10c3): `components/StoryQrCard.tsx`, `lib/storyQr.ts` and `lib/downloadQr.ts`; drawn in the browser, nothing stored.
- `components/BiblePage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/world/:entryId?`, shown as "Story world" (ATLAS-d, S1-E17-17 and 18; renamed from the story bible on 2 Nov 2026, ADMIN-2a2, while the code keeps "bible"; `OldBibleAddressRedirect` keeps old `/bible` links working): `BibleContents`, `BibleEntryView` (reading with `BibleMarkdown` and `CrossLinkLine`, editing with `BibleEntryEditor`, Delete entry in `EntryMoreMenu`; redrawn by user story 6.8, board E27) and `BibleSectionsManager` with `BibleSectionRow`. `hooks/useStoryBible.ts` reads and changes it through `api/bible.ts`; `lib/bibleTags.ts` reads and writes the tags box; `TwoStepDelete` is the shared delete that asks twice.
- The writing view (ATLAS-e, S1-E17-23 to 26), shown inside the chapter editor: `components/WritingChapterList.tsx`, `ChapterPlanPanel.tsx` (CALM-9, board D05: the summary, `ChapterStatusAndGoal` with the goal's meter, `PresentCharacterChips` with a "+ Add" chip, then `ChapterPlanMoreDetails` folding `ChapterPlanBasics`, `ChapterPlanSetting`, `ChapterScenesSection` and `ChapterSceneRow`), `ChapterWordsLine.tsx` and `FocusModeToggle.tsx`; `CharacterAppearancesSection.tsx` on each sheet (S1-E17-12). `api/chapterPlans.ts` with `hooks/useChapterPlan.ts`, `useChapterProgress.ts` and `useCharacterAppearances.ts`; `lib/optionalNumbers.ts` (the target and sort boxes) and `lib/chapterLength.ts` (the long-chapter warning). `api/checkedAnswer.ts` is the shared check on every change's answer.
- `components/StorylinePage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/storyline` (ATLAS-f, S1-E17-19 to 22): `ChapterBoard` (with `BoardColumnSection`, `BoardCard`, `BoardCardFace`, `BoardCardMenu` and `BoardMoveDialog`; dragging is dnd-kit through `hooks/useBoardDrag.ts` and `lib/boardDrag.ts`, CALM-9 board E28), `TimelineView` (with `TimelineRow`, `TimelineChapterItem`, `TimelineEventItem`, `AddEventForm` and `UndatedChapters`; redrawn by user story 6.10, board E29) and `PovLanesView` (a counts line and a plain table, "tells it" under the teller; redrawn by user story 6.11, board E30); `RowMoreMenu` is the shared "…" menu button. `api/storyline.ts` with `hooks/useStoryline.ts`, `useBoardMoves.ts` and `useChapterLabels.ts`; `lib/boardColumns.ts`, `lib/timeline.ts`, `lib/povLanes.ts` and `lib/storylineView.ts`.
- Acts on the board (GAPS-2a): `components/AddActForm.tsx` ("Add act") and `components/EditActForm.tsx` ("Edit act": rename, or delete after a question; the chapters move to "No act"), through `useStoryline`. `hooks/useOneAtATime.ts` runs one change at a time for both; a name another act has (capitals aside) and a 13th act are refused in plain words.
- Marketing details of the writer's own (GAPS-2b): `components/MarketingDetails.tsx` ("Add detail") and `components/MarketingDetailItem.tsx` (the box, "Edit detail": rename, or delete after a question), through `useStoryMarketing`; the back cover's byline is in `BackCoverPreview`. One change runs at a time; a name another note or detail has, and a 13th detail, are refused in plain words.
- Family words (GAPS-3): the Gender drop-down in `components/CharacterIdentityFields.tsx`; `lib/familyWords.ts` (which sentence a parent or sibling gets); `components/FamilyTreeDrawing.tsx`, `components/FamilyTreeLines.tsx` and `components/FamilyTreeGenerations.tsx` (the tree's cards, lines and the same tree in words, CALM-9 and user story 6.7, board E26); `hooks/useCardMeta.ts` (the "b. 1948 · ex-miner" line on map and tree cards).
- `components/IdeasPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/ideas` (ATLAS-g, S1-E17-27 and 28; redrawn as board E31, user stories bundle 6, story 6.12, with the bin's Undo through `restore_story_idea()`): `IdeaQuickAdd` and `IdeaItem`, with `api/ideas.ts`, `hooks/useStoryIdeas.ts` and `lib/ideaFilters.ts`. `components/MarketingPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/marketing` (S1-E17-29): `MarketingFields` and `BackCoverPreview`, with `api/marketing.ts`, `hooks/useStoryMarketing.ts` and `hooks/useStoryChoiceOptions.ts`.
- `components/AtlasSearchPage.tsx`: `/plan/s/:storySlug/search` (ATLAS-h, S1-E17-30), fed by `AtlasSearchBox` in the top bar: `SearchResultItem` and `HighlightedSnippet`, with `api/search.ts`, `hooks/useStorySearch.ts`, `lib/searchGroups.ts`, `lib/highlightMatches.ts` and `lib/itemAddress.ts`. `[[Name]]` cross-links (S1-E17-31): `lib/crossLinks.ts`, `api/links.ts`, `hooks/useLinkTargets.ts`, `hooks/useOpenStorySlug.ts`, `CrossLink`, `CrossLinkText` and `CrossLinkChips`; "Mentioned in" (S1-E17-12) is `CharacterMentionsList` with `hooks/useCharacterMentions.ts`. `hooks/useScrollToHash.ts` brings an item opened from a result into view.
- What readers can see (ATLAS-i, S1-E17-32 to S1-E17-35): the writer's side is `ReaderViewPage` (`/plan/s/:storySlug/readers`) with `PublicAreaSwitches`, `ItemVisibilityControls`, `SheetPartsControls`, `ReaderVisibilitySection` and `PlanPreviewPage`, through `api/readerView.ts`, `hooks/usePlanVisibility.ts` and `hooks/usePlanPreview.ts`. The readers' side is `PublicPlanPage` (`/s/:storySlug/atlas/…`) with `PublicPlanView`, `PublicPlanNav`, `PublicCast`, `PublicCharacter`, `PublicConnections`, `PublicWorld`, `PublicWorldEntry` (one world entry a page with "More from the world", GAPS-4), `PublicTimeline`, `PlanGuardPill` and `MaybeLink`, through `api/publicPlan.ts`, `hooks/usePublicPlan.ts`, `hooks/useCrossLinks.ts`, `lib/publicPlanAreas.ts` and `lib/worldEntries.ts`. Since G10c4 the Readers page also lists each character (`ReaderCharactersList`, `ReaderCharacterRow`, `lib/readerSummary.ts`), and readers' pages and the preview share `PublicPlanHero`, which since GAPS-4 shows the story's cover and the writer's name.
- `registerAtlasTranslations.ts`: the `atlas` text, added when Story Atlas first renders.
- The database side: `supabase/README.md`, "Stories", "Story Atlas plans", "Story Atlas characters", "Story Atlas relationships", "Story Atlas bible" and "Story Atlas chapter planning", "Story Atlas storyline", "Story Atlas questions and ideas, and marketing" and "Story Atlas search and cross-links".
