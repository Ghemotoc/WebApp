# Avatars

The drawn avatar: a person builds it in Settings › Profile and it shows over their initials on their profile and in messages. Built by bundle ADMIN-2, part g (Robert's brief of 2 Nov 2026, section 7). Avatars are never uploaded (Robert, 2 Nov 2026): they are built here or are the initials, so they need no picture check. The art is DiceBear's Micah ("Avatar Illustration System" by Micah Lanier, CC BY 4.0, so the editor credits it), drawn close up in the browser; why is in `docs/adr/0016-avatar-art-micah.md` (since bundle AVATAR-1, 6 Nov 2026; it replaced Open Peeps, ADR 0013).

- `lib/avatarOptions.ts`: every choice the builder offers, part by part, and where it starts (no beard, glasses or earrings: nothing assumes a gender) and which tab each part sits on. `lib/randomRecipe.ts`: "Surprise me". `lib/drawAvatar.ts`: a recipe to an SVG `data:` address; the art loads only when the first avatar is drawn.
- `api/avatarRecipe.ts`: `profile_avatar_recipe()` and `set_my_avatar_recipe()` (`…_admin2_g_avatar_builder.sql`; the database checks the shape with `avatar_recipe_valid()`, Micah since `…_avatar1_01_micah_art.sql`, which also moved the old avatars over). The recipe's shape is the zod schema `src/schemas/avatarRecipe.ts`.
- `hooks/useAvatarRecipe.ts` (a profile's recipe; saving one's own), `hooks/useAvatarDraft.ts` (the draft and Undo), `hooks/useAvatarDrawing.ts` (draws a recipe once and keeps it).
- `components/AvatarBuilderSection.tsx` (Settings: preview, `AvatarEditorTabs`, `AvatarBuilderActions`, `AvatarArtCredit`); `AvatarEditorTabs.tsx` puts each tab's parts on screen as `AvatarPartTiles.tsx` (picture tiles) or `AvatarColourSwatches.tsx` (colour swatches), using the shared `src/components/ui/Tabs.tsx`, `AvatarDrawing.tsx` (one drawn avatar), `AvatarImage.tsx` (a profile's avatar over its initials, used by `ProfileAvatar` and `PersonAvatar`) and `PersonAvatar.tsx` (the one small round avatar beside a name in posts, comments, chats and lists, LOOK-7).
- Text: the `avatars` namespace in `src/locales/{ro,en}/avatars.json`, registered when an avatar component first renders (`registerAvatarTranslations.ts`); staff can override it from Interface text.
