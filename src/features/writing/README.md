# Writing

The writer's side: creating a story, its cover, and (later in the lane) chapters, publishing and managing them. Started by S1-E3-01.

- `components/YourWritingPage.tsx`: `/write` (board 4.1, "Story by story"): the writer's own stories as `WriterStoryCard`s, with "+ New story". The writer dashboard (S1-E3-07) joins it: `WriterDashboardTop` (`DashboardTotals`: the five tiles, the period and the "totals only" note; `FollowersChart`; `NotPublishedYet`), a `ChapterStatsTable` in each `WriterStoryCard`, `LatestComments` and "Continue draft". `hooks/useWriterDashboard.ts` reads `writer_dashboard()` through `api/dashboard.ts`; `lib/dashboardNumbers.ts` writes the numbers ("fewer than 5" for a null count) and `lib/dashboardChapters.ts` works out each chapter's state and the waiting list.
- `components/NewStoryPage.tsx`: `/write/new` (board 4.2): the form sections `StoryBasicsSection` (title, blurb, language), `CategoryTagsSection` (category chips, `TagInput`), `CoverSection` (`DrawnCoverPicker`, `CoverColourSwatches`, the automatic-cover box) and the action bar, with `StoryPreviewAside` beside them. `FormSection` is the card each section sits in.
- `components/StorySettingsPage.tsx`: `/write/s/:storySlug/settings` (S1-E3-05): the same sections filled from the story, `StoryStatusSection` (Ongoing | Completed, the comments switch), the save bar and `DeleteStoryControl` (asked twice, undo). `RecentlyDeleted` on Your writing lists deleted stories with undo.
- `components/ChapterEditorPage.tsx` (S1-E3-02a): `/write/s/:storySlug/c/:chapterCode` (board 4.3): the breadcrumb, the title, `EditorToolbar` (with `EditorToolbarIcons`), the TipTap text, `LinkDialog`, `EditorAside` (`SaveStatus`, Publish, Story details, Your stories). Hooks: `useChapterDraft` (the chapter, owner only), `useChapterEditor` (TipTap with `lib/editorExtensions.ts`), `useAutosave` (every 10 s and on blur → `save_chapter_draft()`). `lib/chapterMarkdown.ts` converts between the stored Markdown subset and the editor's document (ADR 0005); `src/schemas/chapter.ts` holds the rules. S1-E3-02b adds the device copy (`lib/draftStore.ts`, `hooks/useDeviceKeep.ts`, `hooks/useDeviceDraft.ts`), paste cleaning (`lib/pasteCleaning.ts`) and the conflict choices (`CompareVersionsDialog`, the buttons in `EditorAside`). `NewChapterButton` makes chapter one from a story card (and a new chapter from the writing view's list); `lib/storyEditorAddress.ts` says where a story opens. Story Atlas (ATLAS-e) adds the chapter list, the chapter's plan, the words against its target and Focus mode to the editor; those pieces live in `src/features/atlas/`.
- Pictures in chapters (S1-E3-09): `ChapterInsertControls` puts the Picture button and the Insert menu (`InsertMenu`) in `EditorToolbar`, only while the `picture_uploads` flag is on; the Picture button (G9e) opens `PictureInsertDialog` (`PictureFileField`, the alt text, the rights box), which uploads through `src/features/pictures/` and inserts a `chapterPicture` block (`lib/chapterPictureNode.ts`, drawn by `ChapterPictureNodeView` with its check state, the alt text field and "Remove picture"). In the stored text a picture is `![alt](picture:<ID>)` on its own line (`lib/chapterMarkdown.ts`, `src/lib/chapter-pictures.ts`).
- Drawings (S1-E3-11): Insert › Drawing (with `picture_uploads`, since a drawing is saved as a picture) opens `DrawingInsertDialog` with `SketchTools`, `SketchPadCanvas` and `hooks/useSketchPad.ts` (pointer and arrow-key drawing, Undo, Clear); the finished sheet is a PNG that goes through the chapter picture upload and check, and goes in as a `chapterPicture` block. The drawing rules are in `lib/sketchPad.ts`. The Insert dialogs share `hooks/useNativeDialog.ts` and `lib/pictureFailure.ts`.
- Music and video (S1-E3-10): while `media_embeds` is on, the toolbar also has the Video link button and the Insert menu has Music, which open `EmbedInsertDialog` (one link field; the server finds the title and saves the card through `src/features/embeds/`) and insert a `chapterEmbed` block (`lib/chapterEmbedNode.ts`, drawn by `ChapterEmbedNodeView` with "Remove music" or "Remove video"). In the stored text a card is `![title](embed:<ID>)` on its own line (`src/lib/chapter-embeds.ts`).
- `components/PublishPanel.tsx` (S1-E3-03): the editor's Publish panel (now, scheduled in Romania time, published with "Update chapter", unpublish), run by `hooks/useChapterPublishing.ts`; `ChapterPreviewDialog` shows the chapter as readers will. Romania time lives in `src/lib/romania-time.ts`.
- `components/ManageChaptersPage.tsx` (S1-E3-04): `/write/s/:storySlug/chapters` (board 4.4): `ChapterListCard` → `SortableChapterList` (dnd-kit) → `ManagedChapterRow` (`RenameChapterForm`, `ChapterRowActions`, `ManageChaptersIcons`), `RecentlyDeletedChapters` and `DeleteChapterDialog`. `hooks/useManageChapters.ts` runs the list and the changes through `api/manageChapters.ts`; `lib/chapterListText.ts` writes the summary and each row's line. `NewChapterButton` also makes "+ New chapter" here.
- `components/StoryLabelsSection.tsx` (S1-E3-06): "Labels" on both forms: `LabelCheckbox` (fan fiction, parody, abuse or coercion), the content note, the rating chips with `RatingGuide` and "Did you use AI?"; the rules live in `storyLabelsSchema` (`src/schemas/story.ts`) and again in `check_story_labels()`. The pills the labels add to cards and pages are `StoryLabelPills` in `src/features/reading/`.
- `components/WriteLink.tsx`: the top bar's "Write" button.
- Story Atlas (S1-E17, `src/features/atlas/`): "Plan" on each `WriterStoryCard`, "Plan this story" in `EditorAside`, and New story with `?from=plan` opens the new story's plan (`useNewStoryForm`).
- `hooks/useNewStoryForm.ts` (answers, zod checks, `create_story()`), `useStorySettingsForm.ts` (the same for `update_my_story()`), `useStoryActions.ts` (delete and undo), `useStoryFormOptions.ts` (open languages and categories), `useMyStories.ts` (the writer's own list, deleted ones included); `storyAnswers.ts` is the answers type the sections share.
- `api/stories.ts`: `open_story_languages()`, `categories`, `create_story()`, `stories` with tags.
- `components/ChapterInsights.tsx` (IDEAS-1 part d): "Lines and stops" on the dashboard, with `LovedLines` and `ReaderStopsBar`, through `hooks/useChapterInsights.ts` and `api/insights.ts` (`writer_chapter_insights()`). Totals only.
- `components/CardColourField.tsx` (IDEAS-1 part c): the colour readers' new quote and picture cards start in, saved as `card_colour` by `update_my_story()`.
- `lib/tags.ts`: the tag rule the form applies as the writer types, the same as the database's `normalise_tag()`.
- Covers are drawn by `src/components/covers/` (`DrawnCover`, `coverDesigns.ts`, `automaticCover.ts` for a skipped cover, S1-E3-13), shared with every screen that shows a cover. The form makes the story's ID so the automatic design is known before the story exists.
- The database side: `supabase/README.md`, "Stories".
