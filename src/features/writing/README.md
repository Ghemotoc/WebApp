# Writing

The writer's side: creating a story, its cover, and (later in the lane) chapters, publishing and managing them. Started by S1-E3-01.

- `components/YourWritingPage.tsx`: `/write` (board 4.1, "Story by story"): the writer's own stories as `WriterStoryCard`s, with "New story". In the simplified design (CALM-7c, board simplified/Write) each story is a hairline row with one meta line (`lib/writerStoryFacts.ts`) and quiet buttons, so "Continue draft" is the one main button. The writer dashboard (S1-E3-07) joins it: `WriterDashboardTop` (`DashboardTotals`: the figures on the one glass `Hero`, each over a hairline, the period and the "totals only" note; `FollowersChart`, the followers line of CALM-7b: `useFollowersLine` → `api/followersLine.ts` → `writer_followers_line()`, drawn by `FollowersLinePlot` with `lib/followersLineGeometry.ts` and `lib/followersLabels.ts`, its numbers in the hidden `FollowersTable`, and the tooltip moved by `usePointPicker`; `NotPublishedYet`, hairline rows with meta lines), a plain `ChapterStatsTable` (no frame, no pills) in each `WriterStoryCard`, `LatestComments` and "Continue draft". `hooks/useWriterDashboard.ts` reads `writer_dashboard()` through `api/dashboard.ts`; `lib/dashboardNumbers.ts` writes the numbers ("fewer than 5" for a null count) and `lib/dashboardChapters.ts` works out each chapter's state, the waiting list, the all-time comment total and each story's "last updated". Board D01 (user story 5.1) put "Continue draft", "New story" and "Story Atlas" under the intro, opened the figures on the last 30 days, and added the page's two adverts (`PageAdSlot pageKey="write"`, slots `write_wide` and `write_lower`).
- `components/WriteEmptyState.tsx`: `/write` with no live story (board D04, user story 5.4): the "No stories yet" box with "New story" as the page's one main button, "Plan before you write" (a quiet way into the Story Atlas) and `FirstStoryGuides`, the help pages whose address starts with "first-story-" (`lib/firstStoryGuides.ts`, read through `useWebsiteContent`, editable in Staff › Website › Help). `YourWritingPage` shows the figures, followers graph and latest comments only once a chapter is out (`hasChapterOut`).
- `components/ChapterCommentsPage.tsx`: `/write/comments` (GAPS-5, "More comments" on `/write`): every comment readers wrote under the writer's published chapters, newest first, 20 at a time ("Show older"), with a "Story" drop-down once there are two published stories. `WriterCommentCard` is the card it shares with `LatestComments`, which draws it with `look="row"` (a hairline row, CALM-7c); `api/chapterComments.ts` and `hooks/useMyChapterComments.ts` read `my_chapter_comments()`.
- `components/NewStoryPage.tsx`: `/write/new` (board 4.2, redrawn as board D02 in CALM-9): a five-step wizard (`StepWizard` and `useStepWizard` in `src/components/ui/`; the steps in `hooks/useNewStorySteps.tsx`): `StoryBasicsSection` (title, blurb, language), `CategoryTagsSection` (category chips, `SetInCountriesField` for "Set in", `TagInput`), `CoverSection` (`DrawnCoverPicker`, `CoverColourSwatches`, the automatic-cover box), `StoryVisibilitySection` and `StoryLabelsSection`, then the action bar, with `StoryPreviewAside` beside them. `FormSection` is the card each section sits in; inside a wizard step (`InWizardFormSections`) it drops its card. "Set in" is `stories.set_in_countries` (up to five codes), saved by `set_my_story_set_in_countries()`; `saveNewStoryExtras()` saves it and who can read it after `create_story()`. Bundle 5 (story 5.2): `WritingBreadcrumb` heads the page, each step is a box with Back, and Create (`NewStoryActionBar`) sits under the steps from the first one, naming the steps still to finish.
- `components/StorySettingsPage.tsx`: `/write/s/:storySlug/settings` (S1-E3-05): the same sections filled from the story, `StoryStatusSection` (Ongoing | Completed, the comments switch, and "Pause new comments" under Who can comment: `CommentPauseField` with `lib/commentPause.ts`, CALM-8; its switches are `StorySwitch`), `StoryWhereItShowsSection` (Where it shows: the countries where the story is hidden, with the shared `HiddenCountriesPicker` from `src/features/country-block/`, COUNTRY-1), the save bar and `DeleteStoryControl` (board D12: "Delete story" opens `DeleteStoryDialog`, which asks for the title to be typed; then undo). `RecentlyDeleted` on Your writing lists deleted stories with undo.
- `components/ChapterEditorPage.tsx` (S1-E3-02a): `/write/s/:storySlug/c/:chapterCode` (board D05, user story 5.5): `EditorBar` (Back, the title with `SaveWord` and the words, Focus mode, Preview, Publish), `EditorToolbarDock` with `EditorToolbar` (formatting, the "+" `InsertMenu` from `ChapterInsertControls`, Undo, `PoemModeSwitch`), `EditorPaper` (the chapter's number, title, the TipTap text, words and "Saved at"), `SaveStatus` (the live region, shown above the paper only when the writer must act), `EditorStoryDetails` (read-only, linking to Story settings), `LinkDialog`, and Publish in a `Sheet`. Hooks: `useChapterDraft` (the chapter, owner only), `useChapterEditor` (TipTap with `lib/editorExtensions.ts`), `useAutosave` (half a second after typing stops, every 10 s while a save is owed, and on blur → `save_chapter_draft()`). `useIsWideEditor` says whether the window is 1024 px or wider, so the chapter plan is drawn once: in the right-hand column there, in a sheet below (its fields have fixed ids). `lib/chapterMarkdown.ts` converts between the stored Markdown subset and the editor's document (ADR 0005); `src/schemas/chapter.ts` holds the rules. S1-E3-02b adds the device copy (`lib/draftStore.ts`, `hooks/useDeviceKeep.ts`, `hooks/useDeviceDraft.ts`), paste cleaning (`lib/pasteCleaning.ts`) and the conflict choices (`CompareVersionsDialog`, the buttons above the paper and Compare versions in the bar). `NewChapterButton` makes chapter one from a story card (and a new chapter from the writing view's list); Story Atlas (ATLAS-e) adds the chapter list, the chapter's plan, the words against its target and Focus mode to the editor; those pieces live in `src/features/atlas/`.
- Pictures in chapters (S1-E3-09): `ChapterInsertControls` puts the Picture button and the Insert menu (`InsertMenu`) in `EditorToolbar`, only while the `picture_uploads` flag is on; the Picture button (G9e) opens `PictureInsertDialog` (`PictureFileField`, the alt text, the rights box), which uploads through `src/features/pictures/` and inserts a `chapterPicture` block (`lib/chapterPictureNode.ts`, drawn by `ChapterPictureNodeView` with its check state, the alt text field and "Remove picture"). In the stored text a picture is `![alt](picture:<ID>)` on its own line (`lib/chapterMarkdown.ts`, `src/lib/chapter-pictures.ts`).
- Drawings (S1-E3-11): Insert › Drawing (with `picture_uploads`, since a drawing is saved as a picture) opens `DrawingInsertDialog` with `SketchTools`, `SketchPadCanvas` and `hooks/useSketchPad.ts` (pointer and arrow-key drawing, Undo, Clear); the finished sheet is a PNG that goes through the chapter picture upload and check, and goes in as a `chapterPicture` block. The drawing rules are in `lib/sketchPad.ts`. The Insert dialogs share `hooks/useNativeDialog.ts` and `lib/pictureFailure.ts`.
- Music and video (S1-E3-10): while `media_embeds` is on, the toolbar also has the Video link button and the Insert menu has Music, which open `EmbedInsertDialog` (one link field; the server finds the title and saves the card through `src/features/embeds/`) and insert a `chapterEmbed` block (`lib/chapterEmbedNode.ts`, drawn by `ChapterEmbedNodeView` with "Remove music" or "Remove video"). In the stored text a card is `![title](embed:<ID>)` on its own line (`src/lib/chapter-embeds.ts`).
- `components/PublishPanel.tsx` (S1-E3-03): the editor's Publish panel (now, scheduled on the writer's own clock (CALM-8b), published with "Update chapter", unpublish), run by `hooks/useChapterPublishing.ts`; `ChapterPreviewDialog` shows the chapter as readers will. Romania time lives in `src/lib/romania-time.ts`.
- `components/ManageChaptersPage.tsx` (S1-E3-04): `/write/s/:storySlug/chapters` (board 4.4, redrawn as board D03 in user story 5.3): `ChapterStats` (four numbers, `lib/chapterStats.ts`), `ChapterList` (a long story opens on its newest six) → `SortableChapterList` (dnd-kit, the whole row drags; `lib/blockClickAfterDrag.ts`) → `ManagedChapterRow` (`RenameChapterForm`, `ChapterRowActions` with Earlier, Later, Edit and the shared `MoreMenu`, `ManageChaptersIcons`), `RecentlyDeletedChapters` (Restore, days left) and `DeleteChapterDialog`. `hooks/useManageChapters.ts` runs the list and the changes through `api/manageChapters.ts`; `lib/chapterListText.ts` writes the summary, each row's line and the note for a chapter whose number changed ("was chapter 13", GAPS-11; from `chapter_move_notes()`). `NewChapterButton` also makes "+ New chapter" here.
- `components/StoryLabelsSection.tsx` (S1-E3-06): "Labels" on both forms: `LabelCheckbox` (fan fiction, parody, abuse or coercion), the content note, the rating chips with `RatingGuide` and "Did you use AI?"; the rules live in `storyLabelsSchema` (`src/schemas/story.ts`) and again in `check_story_labels()`. The labels show as a meta line: `lib/storyPreviewFacts.ts` on the preview (CALM-9c) and `storyLabelFacts` in `src/features/reading/lib/` on the story page.
- `components/WriteLink.tsx`: the top bar's "Write" button.
- Story Atlas (S1-E17, `src/features/atlas/`): "Plan" on each `WriterStoryCard`, the plan panel's link to the story's plan in the editor, and New story with `?from=plan` opens the new story's plan (`useNewStoryForm`).
- `hooks/useNewStoryForm.ts` (answers, zod checks, `create_story()`), `useStorySettingsForm.ts` (the same for `update_my_story()`), `useStoryActions.ts` (delete and undo), `useStoryFormOptions.ts` (every language of the world and the categories, S1-E3-01), `useStoryCategories.ts` (the categories alone); `components/LanguagePicker.tsx` is the searchable language drop-down, `useMyStories.ts` (the writer's own list, deleted ones included); `storyAnswers.ts` is the answers type the sections share.
- `api/stories.ts`: `categories`, `create_story()`, `stories` with tags (the languages need no read since S1-E3-01: `src/lib/world-languages.ts`).
- `components/ChapterInsights.tsx` (IDEAS-1 part d): "Lines and stops" on the dashboard, with `LovedLines` and `ReaderStopsBar`, through `hooks/useChapterInsights.ts` and `api/insights.ts` (`writer_chapter_insights()`). Totals only.
- `components/CardColourField.tsx` (IDEAS-1 part c): the colour readers' new quote and picture cards start in, saved as `card_colour` by `update_my_story()`.
- `lib/tags.ts`: the tag rule the form applies as the writer types, the same as the database's `normalise_tag()`.
- Covers are drawn by `src/components/covers/` (`DrawnCover`, `coverDesigns.ts`, `automaticCover.ts` for a skipped cover, S1-E3-13), shared with every screen that shows a cover. The form makes the story's ID so the automatic design is known before the story exists.
- The database side: `supabase/README.md`, "Stories".
- Replace picture and Change link (GAPS-8): `PictureReplaceControl` in `ChapterPictureNodeView` and `EmbedLinkChange` in `ChapterEmbedNodeView` run the same upload and check, or the same look-up, as a new insert, then swap the block's ID in place (`updateAttributes`). The blocks read the chapter from `lib/editingChapter.ts` (`EditingChapterContext`, provided by `ChapterEditorPage`); `lib/embedFailure.ts` holds the refused-link messages both the dialog and the card use.
- Editor texts: `registerEditorTranslations.ts` adds the `editor` namespace when an editor component first renders (`useEditorTranslation()`), so the texts travel with the editor's own file and not with every first page (GAPS-6; the reader's script budget in `e2e/reader-performance.spec.ts`).
- Poem mode (MUST-2): `lib/poemMode.ts` (Enter, paste and kept indents), `components/PoemModeSwitch.tsx`, `hooks/usePoemMode.ts`; the switch is stored per chapter in `chapters.is_poem` through `set_chapter_poem_mode()`.
- The last chapter (CALM-6): `components/LastChapterSwitch.tsx` ("This is the last chapter") in `PublishPanel`, for a draft, a scheduled chapter and the latest published one; `api/lastChapter.ts` reads `my_chapter_last_state()` and calls `publish_chapter(code, as_last)` and `set_chapter_last()`, through `hooks/useChapterPublishing.ts`. Tests: `components/PublishPanel.test.tsx`.
- Genres (S1-E3-01, Robert, 26 Nov 2026): `components/GenrePickerField.tsx` (type a few letters, pick from the pop-up list, up to three chips, built like `SetInCountriesField`), `hooks/useGenres.ts` and `api/genres.ts` (the `genres` table, `story_list_genres()` and `genreName()`), `lib/genreChoices.ts` (matching typed text, names, whether an 18+ genre needs the 18+ rating). Saved by `setMyStoryGenres()` in `api/stories.ts` → `set_my_story_genres()`. The database side: `supabase/migrations/…_s1_e3_01_genres.sql`; test `420-genres`.
- Footnotes (BOOKS-D05b, board D13): `lib/chapterFootnoteNode.ts` (the `chapterFootnote` node and Ctrl+Alt+F), `components/ChapterFootnoteNodeView.tsx` (the number in the text), `hooks/useFootnoteEditing.ts` (insert, open, change, remove, close), `components/FootnoteNotePanel.tsx` (the note's box) and `components/EditorFootnoteList.tsx` (Notes under the text); stored as `[note](footnote:)` (`src/lib/chapter-footnotes.ts`).
