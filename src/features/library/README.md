# Library

The reader's library and their place in every chapter. Built by READ-4a: S1-E4-03 (library and continue reading).

- `components/LibraryPage.tsx`: screen 3.7 at `/library`, in the simplified design since CALM-7c (board `design/boards/simplified/New-Library.dc.html`): the header with the privacy line (`LibraryVisibilityLine`, a meta line), ContinueReading in the one hero, LibraryShelves, ReadingListsCard, then two columns from 900 px: FollowingSection on the left; Written by you, OfflineReadingNote and ReadingHabitNote on the right.
- `components/ContinueReadingCard.tsx`: one story to carry on with, a list item in the hero: cover, title, "by … · chapter 3 of 15", the 2 px thread line, where the reader stopped, "Chapter 15 is out" as a meta line, Continue (the main button on the first story only) and Follow story (CALM-7c).
- `components/LibraryShelves.tsx`: "Stories in the library" with a segmented control (Reading, Saved, Finished, each with its size) and the chosen shelf as the shared Shelf (See all at `/library/<shelf>`, the discovery feature's StoryListPage).
- `components/MyStoriesShelf.tsx`: "Written by you", a list of the writer's own stories with drafts (status and visibility as a meta line), each opening its chapters in the writing tools, plus "Open Write" (bundle NAV-1; a list since CALM-7c).
- `components/OfflineReadingNote.tsx`: "Downloaded to read offline", which says offline reading comes with the apps (CALM-7c).
- `components/FollowingSection.tsx`: the writers, stories, genres and tags the reader follows, with the shared FollowButton (genres and tags since BOOKS-A06; the group shows only once something is in it).
- `components/StoryReadingActions.tsx`: the story page's "Start reading" / "Continue" / "Read again", "Add to library" / "In library" (the bookshelf sheet, with the Library drop-down on top) and "Tell the writer you finished it" (bundle 2, board B01).
- `hooks/`, `api/library.ts`: `my_library()`, `my_follows()`, `story_library_state()`, `set_library_shelf()`, `my_place_in_chapter()`, `save_reading_position()`.
- `lib/places.ts` (where Continue leads, "Chapter 15 is out"), `lib/deviceKind.ts` (phone, tablet or computer from the window width).
- The reader screen's side lives in `src/features/reading/`: `hooks/useSavedPlace.ts` and `components/SavedPlaceOffer.tsx`.
- The database side: `supabase/migrations/…_s1_e4_03_library.sql`; test `179`.
- `components/FinishedThanksButton.tsx` and `hooks/useFinishedThanks.ts` (IDEAS-1 part c): "Tell the writer you finished it" on a story on the Finished shelf, through `send_finished_thanks()`; it reads "Sent" once it went.
- `components/ReadingHabitNote.tsx` and `ReadingHabitSettings.tsx` (IDEAS-1 part b): "Reading days", the private reading streak, from `src/features/reading-habit/`.
- Reading lists (GAPS-6; the board's "Your lists"): `components/ReadingListsCard.tsx` (the Library's rows and "New list"), `components/NewReadingListForm.tsx`, `components/ReadingListPage.tsx` at `/library/lists/:listId` with `components/ReadingListOwnerTools.tsx` (rename, who sees it, remove a story, delete), and `components/AddToReadingList.tsx` on the story page. `hooks/useReadingLists.ts`, `api/readingLists.ts`: `my_reading_lists()`, `reading_list()`, `story_reading_lists()`, `create_reading_list()`, `update_reading_list()`, `delete_reading_list()`, `set_story_in_reading_list()`. `lib/readingListAddress.ts`. The database side: `supabase/migrations/…_gaps6_reading_lists.sql`; test `328`.
- Bookshelves (CALM-4): reading lists are called bookshelves in the interface. `components/BookshelfPlaces.tsx` draws one bookshelf's books and ghost places, `components/BookshelfChoiceRow.tsx` is one row of the "Add to a bookshelf" sheet (`components/AddToReadingList.tsx`, on `src/components/ui/Sheet.tsx`), and `hooks/useBookshelfLimits.ts` reads the free plan's limits (3 bookshelves, 10 books each).
