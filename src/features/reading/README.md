# Reading

The reader's side: the story page, the chapter reader and the teaser. Started by S1-E3-05.

- `components/StoryPage.tsx`: `/s/:storySlug` (board 3.4): `StoryHeader` (cover, pills, title, writer, blurb, tags) and `StorySideColumn` (the writer card, "Labels from the writer", "Report this story"). The rating, label and AI pills, the content note (under the blurb) and the Rating, Labels and AI rows are S1-E3-06. The chapter list comes with S1-E3-03; its published titles link to the reader (S1-E4-01a).
- `components/StoryLabelPills.tsx` (S1-E3-06): the pills a story's labels add (13+/16+/18+, Fan fiction, Parody, the AI answer, "AI-written, not disclosed"); the writer's preview and card use it too.
- `components/StoryChapterList.tsx` (S1-E3-03): "Chapters" on the story page; a published chapter's title links to the reader (S1-E4-01a).
- `components/ChapterText.tsx` (S1-E3-03): a chapter's Markdown as reading text (no raw HTML, the allowed elements only, safe links with `rel="nofollow ugc noopener"`); the editor's preview and the reader use it (the reader adds `tmc-chapter-text--reader`, 19 px unless the reader picks another size).
- `components/ChapterReaderPage.tsx` (S1-E4-01a): `/s/:storySlug/:chapterCode` (board 3.5): `ChapterBar` (back to the story, "Chapter n of N", Aa Reading settings, Contents, Previous, Next chapter, the progress line; a glass bar since G7d), the chapter on a sheet of paper (`ReadingPaper`), `ReadingTimeLeft` ("3 min left" floating at the bottom, `lib/readingTimeLeft.ts`), `ChapterReaderHeader` ("Chapter n of N", the H1, "by … · date · n min read"), the text, `ChapterEnd` ("End of chapter n", "Read chapter n+1"). No doodles. Hooks: `useChapter` (`get_chapter()`), `useRecordRead` (`record_chapter_read()`, once per chapter per tab), `useReadingProgress`. Icons in `ReaderIcons`. S1-E4-01b: `api/chapterPrefetch.ts` (the route's loader starts `get_chapter()` early), `ChapterBannerSlot` and `hooks/useChapterBanner.ts` (the banner below the chapter, empty until S1-E12-01); `e2e/reader-performance.spec.ts` keeps the reader within its 2-second and 40 KB budgets. S1-E4-06a: `ProtectedChapterText` (right-click, copy, cut and drag off on the text only) and `CopyNotice` ("makes copying harder", Q9); `copyWording.test.ts` scans every locale file for promises like "can't be copied".
- `components/TeaserPage.tsx` (S1-E4-04): what a signed-out visitor gets at `/s/:storySlug/:chapterCode` (board 3.8): `TeaserStoryCard` (cover, "Shared with you · chapter n of N", the story's title as the H1, pen name, blurb, pills), the chapter's heading and the teaser text (cut on the server, fading out when the chapter goes on), `SignUpPrompt` (the March "log in to read on" card) and the copy notice. `NoPreviewNotice`: the title and "No preview for this story" for 16+ and 18+ stories, on the reader and the story page, through `hooks/useStoryPreview.ts` and `api/storyPreview.ts` (`get_link_preview()`).
- The shared link's HTML (Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, noindex) isn't made here: `server/link-preview.ts`, run by `functions/s/[[path]].ts` on Cloudflare.
- `hooks/useStoryPage.ts`: the page, cached per address and per person.
- `api/chapter.ts` (S1-E4-01a): `get_chapter()` and `record_chapter_read()`.
- `api/storyPage.ts`: `get_story_page()`, the only way readers reach a story (ADR 0004): nothing for an unpublished, deleted or moderated-away story, or one rated above the reader's age (`rating_allows()`, S1-E3-06); never the user ID.
- `components/LikeButton.tsx`, `hooks/useChapterLike.ts`, `api/likes.ts` (S1-E6-03): "Like this chapter" / "Liked" with the public count, first in `ChapterEnd`'s row (`chapter_like_state()`, `like_chapter()`). The row ends with the shared `ReportButton` ("Report chapter", by the chapter's row ID from `get_chapter()`).
- Reading settings (S1-E4-02): `ReadingSettingsControl` (the chapter bar's button, signed-in readers only) opens `ReadingSettingsPanel`, loaded only when first opened to keep the reader under its 40 KB script budget, (font, `TextSizeSlider`, line spacing, line width, theme). `hooks/useReadingSettings.ts` reads `reading_settings` and saves through `save_reading_settings()` 600 ms after the last change (`api/readingSettings.ts`); `hooks/useReadingLook.ts` and `lib/readingStyle.ts` turn them, with the device's own text size, into CSS variables on the text (`.tmc-chapter-text--reader`) and the paper the chapter sits on (`.tmc-reading-page`, in every theme since G7d).
- Sharing (S1-E4-05, S1-E4-07): `ChapterEnd` shows `ShareButton` after Like and `ChapterReaderPage` shows `QuoteShareWatcher` (which fetches `QuoteShareBar` on the first selection), both from `src/features/sharing/`; `lib/toSharedChapter.ts` gives them the chapter's public details only.
- The database side: `supabase/README.md`, "Stories".
- Shared passages (S1-E4-11, WRITE-5e): `lib/numberBlocks.ts` marks each part of the chapter text with its blocks and highlights a shared passage; `components/SharedPassageNotice.tsx` brings it into view at `/s/:storySlug/:chapterCode/:spot`. The Share buttons themselves live in `src/features/sharing/`.
- Paragraph comments (S1-E6-06, WRITE-5f): `components/ParagraphCommentMarker.tsx` beside each paragraph (ChapterText's `paragraphAddon`), `components/EarlierVersionComments.tsx` after the text, `hooks/useParagraphCommentCounts.ts` → `paragraph_comment_counts()`, and `lib/paragraphCommentsContext.ts` carrying the counts in. The thread is fetched from `src/features/comments/` (`components/LazyParagraphThread.tsx`). From 1280 px it opens in the side column beside the paper (`components/ParagraphCommentsPanel.tsx`, `hooks/useIsWideReader.ts`; bundle G7d2).
- Reading place and comfort (IDEAS-1 part a): "You are here" in `StoryChapterList` (a link ending `#resume`, which `SavedPlaceOffer` follows at once); the Atkinson Hyperlegible font; Night by the clock (`hooks/useChapterTheme.ts`, `lib/nightByClock.ts`); the reading ruler (`components/ReadingRuler.tsx`, `lib/rulerLines.ts`); the panel's new fields are `components/ReadingComfortFields.tsx`.
- `hooks/useLinkedParagraphThread.ts` (IDEAS-1 part e): a chapter address ending `#paragraph-4` or `#paragraph-earlier` opens that thread and brings its marker into view with focus.
- Poems (MUST-2): `lib/poemStanzas.ts` draws each paragraph of a poem as a named stanza and each line as its own block with a hanging indent (`ChapterText`'s `isPoem`, styled by `.tmc-poem`).
