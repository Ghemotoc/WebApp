# Reading

The reader's side: the story page, the chapter reader and the teaser. Started by S1-E3-05.

- `components/StoryPage.tsx`: `/s/:storySlug` (board 3.4; since CALM-7c board New-Story): `StoryHeader` (the page's one glass hero: cover, the label meta line from `lib/storyLabelFacts.ts`, title, writer, numbers, blurb, tags, buttons), then `StoryChapterList`, `StoryWorldLinks` and `StoryLabels` ("Labels from the writer" and the content note, S1-E3-06) in the main column and `StorySideColumn` (the writer, the advert in `StoryAdSlot`, "Report this story") beside them. `lib/storyPageNumbers.ts` counts the words and says which chapters are new. While the writer has paused new comments, the Comments row says until when, in the reader's own time (CALM-8, CALM-8b).
- `lib/storyLabelFacts.ts` (CALM-7c): a story's labels (13+/16+/18+, Fan fiction, Parody, the AI answer, "AI-written, not disclosed") as the story page's meta line. The writer's preview uses `storyPreviewFacts` in `src/features/writing/lib/` (CALM-9c, which retired `StoryLabelPills`).
- `components/StoryChapterList.tsx` (S1-E3-03): the chapters on the story page ("15 chapters", two columns from 900 px, the state in words; CALM-7c); a published chapter's title links to the reader (S1-E4-01a).
- `components/ChapterText.tsx` (S1-E3-03): a chapter's Markdown as reading text (no raw HTML, the allowed elements only, safe links with `rel="nofollow ugc noopener"`); the editor's preview and the reader use it (the reader adds `tmc-chapter-text--reader`, 19 px unless the reader picks another size).
- `components/ChapterReaderPage.tsx` (S1-E4-01a): `/s/:storySlug/:chapterCode` (board 3.5; the simplified design since CALM-5, boards New-Reader and New-PhoneReader). `ChapterReading` draws the screen: `ChapterBar` (Back to the story, the story's title, "Chapter n of N · 3 min left", Contents (`ChapterContentsControl` and `ChapterContentsPlace`, user story 2.2), Aa, a quiet Next chapter from 768 px; on phones the only bar, through `src/app/shell/pageHeaderContext.ts`), the chapter on a sheet of paper (`ReadingPaper`, full width on phones), `ChapterReaderHeader` ("Chapter n of N", the H1 in Literata 600, "by … · date · n min read"), the text, `ChapterEnd` ("End of chapter n of N", "Next: chapter n+1", Like, "Comment on the chapter", Share and Report), `ChapterNavigation` (Previous, the dots from `lib/chapterDots.ts`, Next), the adverts and the comments. From 1280 px `ReaderSideColumn` holds Contents or Reading settings when open, `AboutThisStory`, a paragraph's comments when open (level with the paragraph, `hooks/useThreadLevelWithParagraph.ts`, CALM-9 B03) and the 300 × 250 advert; below that the panels open in `ReaderSheet`. `ReadingTimeLeft` floats "3 min left" only for a reader who chose "On the page" (`lib/readingTimeLeft.ts`). No doodles. Hooks: `useChapter` (`get_chapter()`), `useRecordRead` (`record_chapter_read()`, once per chapter per tab), `useReadingProgress`. Icons in `ReaderIcons`. S1-E4-01b: `api/chapterPrefetch.ts` (the route's loader starts `get_chapter()` early), `ChapterBannerSlot` and `hooks/useChapterBanner.ts` (the banner below the chapter, empty until S1-E12-01); `e2e/reader-performance.spec.ts` keeps the reader within its 2-second and 40 KB budgets. S1-E4-06a: `ProtectedChapterText` (right-click, copy, cut and drag off on the text only) and `CopyNotice` ("makes copying harder", Q9); `copyWording.test.ts` scans every locale file for promises like "can't be copied".
- `components/TeaserPage.tsx` (S1-E4-04): what a signed-out visitor gets at `/s/:storySlug/:chapterCode` (board 3.8): `TeaserStoryCard` (cover, "Shared with you · chapter n of N", the story's title as the H1, pen name, blurb, pills), the chapter's heading and the teaser text (cut on the server, fading out when the chapter goes on), `SignUpPrompt` (the March "log in to read on" card) and the copy notice. `NoPreviewNotice`: the title and "No preview for this story" for 16+ and 18+ stories, on the reader and the story page, through `hooks/useStoryPreview.ts` and `api/storyPreview.ts` (`get_link_preview()`).
- The shared link's HTML (Open Graph tags, JSON-LD, noindex) isn't made here: `server/link-preview.ts`, run by `functions/s/[[path]].ts` on Cloudflare.
- `hooks/useStoryPage.ts`: the page, cached per address and per person.
- `api/chapter.ts` (S1-E4-01a): `get_chapter()` and `record_chapter_read()`.
- `api/storyPage.ts`: `get_story_page()`, the only way readers reach a story (ADR 0004): nothing for an unpublished, deleted or moderated-away story, or one rated above the reader's age (`rating_allows()`, S1-E3-06); never the user ID.
- `components/LikeButton.tsx`, `hooks/useChapterLike.ts`, `api/likes.ts` (S1-E6-03): "Like this chapter" / "Liked" with the public count, first in `ChapterEnd`'s row (`chapter_like_state()`, `like_chapter()`). The row ends with the shared `ReportButton` ("Report chapter", by the chapter's row ID from `get_chapter()`).
- Reading settings (S1-E4-02): `ReadingSettingsControl` (Aa in the chapter bar, for everyone who reaches the reader) opens `ReadingSettingsPanel` through `ReaderToolPlace` (the side column or the sheet; `hooks/useReaderTools.ts` keeps it and a paragraph's comments from being open together, CALM-5), loaded only when first opened to keep the reader under its script budget (redrawn to board B04-ReaderSettings in BOOKS B04, user story 2.4: Paper, Night mode, Typeface, `TextSizeSlider` and Time left from `ReadingSettingsChoices.tsx`, then a closed More options with the comfort fields, line spacing and line width). `hooks/useReadingSettings.ts` reads `reading_settings` and saves through `save_reading_settings()` 600 ms after the last change (`api/readingSettings.ts`); signed out, it keeps them in the browser (`lib/browserReadingSettings.ts`); `hooks/useReadingLook.ts` and `lib/readingStyle.ts` turn them, with the device's own text size, into CSS variables on the text (`.tmc-chapter-text--reader`) and the paper the chapter sits on (`.tmc-reading-page`: White, Sepia, Grey or Black paper since BOOKS B04).
- Sharing (S1-E4-05, S1-E4-07): `ChapterEnd` shows `ShareButton` after Like and `ChapterReaderPage` shows `QuoteShareWatcher` (which fetches `QuoteShareBar` on the first selection), both from `src/features/sharing/`; `lib/toSharedChapter.ts` gives them the chapter's public details only.
- The database side: `supabase/README.md`, "Stories".
- Shared passages (S1-E4-11, WRITE-5e): `lib/numberBlocks.ts` marks each part of the chapter text with its blocks and highlights a shared passage; `components/SharedPassageNotice.tsx` brings it into view at `/s/:storySlug/:chapterCode/:spot`. The Share buttons themselves live in `src/features/sharing/`.
- Paragraph comments (S1-E6-06, WRITE-5f): `components/ParagraphCommentMarker.tsx` beside each paragraph (ChapterText's `paragraphAddon`; in the margin from 1024 px and at the paragraph's corner below that, CALM-5; during a pause of new comments, CALM-8, a paragraph without comments has no button), `components/EarlierVersionComments.tsx` after the text, `hooks/useParagraphCommentCounts.ts` → `paragraph_comment_counts()`, and `lib/paragraphCommentsContext.ts` carrying the counts in. The thread is fetched from `src/features/comments/` (`components/LazyParagraphThread.tsx`). From 1280 px it opens in the side column beside the paper (`components/ReaderSideColumn.tsx`, `hooks/useIsWideReader.ts`), below that in the bottom sheet (`components/ReaderSheet.tsx`; CALM-5).
- Reading place and comfort (IDEAS-1 part a): "You are here" in `StoryChapterList` (a link ending `#resume`, which `SavedPlaceOffer` follows at once); the Atkinson Hyperlegible font; Night by the clock (`hooks/useChapterPaper.ts`, `lib/nightByClock.ts`; it turns the chapter Black); the reading ruler (`components/ReadingRuler.tsx`, `lib/rulerLines.ts`); the panel's new fields are `components/ReadingComfortFields.tsx`.
- `hooks/useLinkedParagraphThread.ts` (IDEAS-1 part e): a chapter address ending `#paragraph-4` or `#paragraph-earlier` opens that thread and brings its marker into view with focus.
- Poems (MUST-2): `lib/poemStanzas.ts` draws each paragraph of a poem as a named stanza and each line as its own block with a hanging indent (`ChapterText`'s `isPoem`, styled by `.tmc-poem`).
- One story in several languages (TRANSLATE-1b; Robert, 16 Nov 2026): `api/editions.ts` (the editions `get_story_page()` and `get_chapter()` answer, and `story_opening_edition()`), `components/ReadInSwitch.tsx` (the "Read in" pill, drawn only for a story in more than one language), `hooks/useOpeningEdition.ts` and `lib/rememberedEdition.ts` (the story opens in the page's language; a language the reader chose is remembered in this browser), `components/StoryLanguageNote.tsx` (the note under the description, the translator credit, "About this edition") and `components/MissingTranslationNotice.tsx` (a chapter not translated yet). The database side: `supabase/migrations/…_translate1_01_story_editions.sql`; tests `382` to `384` and `e2e/story-languages.spec.ts`. BOOKS B07 (user story 2.7, board B07): the list's rows are one line ("English · Translated by …", "Română · Original"), the reader keeps the paragraph across a change of language (`lib/languageSwitchPlace.ts`) and says so with `components/LanguageSwitchNote.tsx`, and under 768 px the pill has its own row under the chapter bar. Part 1d adds the "Rights" line to "About this edition" for a translated classic (public domain, licensed, or translated for the platform; `story_edition_json()`'s `translation_rights`).
- The last chapter (CALM-6): `lib/chapterPlace.ts` writes "Chapter 14 of 15" only while the writer has marked a last chapter (`lastChapterNumber` from `get_chapter()` and `get_story_page()`), else "Chapter 14", and "Next chapter Thursday" or "Last chapter Thursday" from `nextChapter` (the day from `chapterDayWording()` in `src/lib/local-time.ts`). `ChapterEnd` says "That's the end of the story." over "Completed · n chapters" after the marked chapter, with "More by …" (the writer's stories page) as the main button and no Back to the story; `StoryEnd` then puts "What next" under the paper: a green note that the story is complete with Follow for the writer, then the suggestions (BOOKS B06, user story 2.6; `isStoryEnd()` in `lib/chapterPlace.ts`); `AboutThisStory` and `StoryChapterList` show the next chapter's day.
