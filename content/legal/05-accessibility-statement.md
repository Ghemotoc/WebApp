# Accessibility statement

**Draft for lawyer review, 17 October 2026**

> This is a first draft for a Romanian lawyer (avocat) to review before launch. It is not final and it is not legal advice.

## In short

- We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA.
- Test results and known gaps are added after our first audit, before launch.
- Tell us about any barrier and a person will reply.
- No tracking cookies or outside tracking scripts.

## 1. Our target

**In short** (as on the board)

- We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, on the web and in the apps.

*Suggested new short line, now that the full text fills the placeholder:*

- We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA on the website and web app now, and in the apps when they arrive.

**Full text**

We want everyone to be able to read and write on Ghemotoc, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, zoom, voice control or other assistive technology.

Our target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This statement covers the Ghemotoc website and web app at [web address]. The Ghemotoc apps come after launch. When they do, they will aim for the same level and this statement will cover them too.

What we do to get there:

- every screen works by keyboard alone, with a visible focus;
- every button and field has a name screen readers can read, and every picture has alt text (writers must add it when they upload one);
- text and buttons meet WCAG colour contrast, in light and night mode;
- text is real text, never a picture of text, including the titles on story covers;
- every change is checked with automatic tests, with a screen reader and at 200% zoom before it goes live.

The law. The European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882, in Romania Law 232/2022) has applied since 28 June 2025 to some services sold to consumers, such as online shops and ebooks. Micro-enterprises that provide services are exempt. [Lawyer to confirm whether the Act applies to Ghemotoc] We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA anyway, because many of our readers and writers are young, and everyone should be able to take part.

This statement was prepared on [date] and last reviewed on [date].

## 5. No trackers

**In short** (as on the board)

- We use no tracking cookies or outside tracking scripts.
- Every new script or stored item is checked before it goes live.

**Full text**

We use no tracking cookies and no outside tracking scripts. This matters for accessibility too: fewer scripts mean faster pages and fewer pop-ups getting in the way of screen readers and keyboards.

There’s no cookie banner, because we only store what Ghemotoc needs to work, such as keeping you signed in. Music and video from other services load only when you press play.

Every new script, cookie or stored item is checked automatically before it goes live. If it isn’t on our approved list, it doesn’t ship. Details are in our Privacy notice, under “Cookies and storage”.

## Drafting notes for the lawyer

- Review the statement at least once a year and after every big change (proposal).
- Section 1 (Our target): WCAG 2.2 AA from the first screen (CTO pack principle 8); the Definition of Done accessibility checklist (axe, keyboard, accessible names, contrast 4.5:1 and 3:1, 24 px targets, one screen reader, 200% zoom) is described in plain words.
- Section 1 (Our target): March 2027 is web only (decided 17 Oct 2026, 34o), so the board line “on the web and in the apps” is out of date for version 0.1; a summary update is suggested.
- Section 1 (Our target): EAA: Directive (EU) 2019/882; Law 232/2022 (Monitorul Oficial nr. 743, 25 July 2022); micro-enterprise exemption for services (fewer than 10 employees and turnover or balance sheet not above EUR 2 million) (research A2, section 8; S1-E14-03). Ghemotoc sells nothing to consumers at launch, so the Act may not reach it; please confirm.
- Section 1 (Our target): Possible reference to EN 301 549 (the harmonised European standard) left out because the research files do not cover which version applies.
- Section 2 (What we tested): Kept as a placeholder: no audit has happened. Never state a compliance level before an audit.
- Section 2 (What we tested): The Yoti age check runs on Yoti’s hosted page: include it in the audit scope or name it as a third-party part.
- Section 3 (Known gaps): Check in the audit: switching off right-click and copying on chapter text (S1-E4-06, Q35) must not block screen readers, zoom, text size or text-to-speech tools (WCAG 2.2; L17). If any assistive tool is affected, list it here.
- Section 3 (Known gaps): Third-party parts named are those in the pack (S1-E2-08, S1-E3-10, S1-E2-09).
- Section 4 (Tell us about a barrier): Contact for problems: S1-E14-03. Reply time not decided: placeholder (proposal for the founder: 5 working days).
- Section 4 (Tell us about a barrier): Enforcement route: Law 232/2022 splits market surveillance between ANPC, the Ministry of Economy, ANCOM and the Autoritatea pentru Digitalizarea României by category; the exact allocation for services like Ghemotoc was not read (research A2, section 8). If the Act doesn’t apply, a general consumer complaint route is the fallback.
- Section 5 (No trackers): CI no-cookie and no-third-party-script check: S1-E14-03. Click-to-load embeds: S1-E3-10.
