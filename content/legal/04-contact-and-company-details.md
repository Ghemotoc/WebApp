This is our contact point for everyone who uses Ghemotoc.

Questions, problems or complaints: email hello@themagiccoffin.com. You don’t need an account.

Logged in? Use Contact us in Settings, Help or the footer. Every message gets a reference number and an email to confirm we’ve got it. You can follow every request in Settings, under “Your support requests”.

A person answers. Messages are read and answered by our team, not only by an automatic system. We aim to reply within [number] working days.

Reporting content? Please use the Report button or the public report form instead, so it reaches our moderators straight away.

Languages: Romanian and English.

## 3. Terrorist content removal orders

**In short** (as on the board)

- Send removal orders through our report form: choose “Removal order from an authority”.
- We act on a removal order within one hour of receiving it.
- Languages: Romanian and English.

**Full text**

Contact point for removal orders under Regulation (EU) 2021/784 on addressing the dissemination of terrorist content online (article 15).

How to send an order: use our report form and choose “Removal order from an authority”. No account is needed. The form confirms receipt straight away, with the time we received it. If the form is unavailable, email [authorities email].

Within one hour. We remove the content, or disable access to it in every EU country, within one hour of receiving a removal order. Someone from our team is on call at all times to do this.

After removal, we keep the removed content and related data for six months, as the Regulation requires, and we tell the person who posted it unless you tell us not to.

Languages: Romanian and English.

## 5. Report illegal content

**In short** (as on the board)

- Anyone can report, with or without an account, using the public report form.

**Full text**

Anyone can report content they think is illegal or breaks our rules, with or without an account, using the public report form. Signed in? Use the Report button next to the content.

For a report about illegal content, the form asks for the exact link, why you think it’s illegal, your name and email and a statement that you’re reporting in good faith. You don’t need to give your name for reports about child sexual abuse.

We confirm we got your report, a person reads it and we tell you what we decided.

If someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number first (112 in Romania and across the EU). The report form shows the emergency and helpline numbers for your country.

How we handle reports: see our Notice and action policy.

## Drafting notes for the lawyer

- Points of contact must be easily accessible and kept up to date (DSA art. 11(2), 12(2)); the app needs the same page one tap from Settings (S1-E14-02).
- Section 1 (For users): DSA art. 12: single point of contact for users, chosen by electronic means and user-friendly, not solely automated (art. 12(1)). Support tickets: S1-E11-13 (17 Oct 2026); Contact us: S1-E14-04.
- Section 1 (For users): First-reply target is a proposal of one working day in S1-E11-13, not decided: placeholder.
- Section 2 (For authorities): DSA art. 11(1) to (3): designate and publish a single point of contact for authorities, the Commission and the Board, with the languages accepted (an official language of the Member State of establishment and a language broadly understood). Art. 9(1) and 10(1): inform the authority of the effect given to orders.
- Section 2 (For authorities): Law 50/2024 art. 5(1): notify ANCOM within 45 days of starting the service, including this contact point (L18). Changes within 10 days (art. 5(3)).
- Section 2 (For authorities): Monitored email plus the form: proposal in S1-E10-02 and S1-E14-02. Whether a web form alone satisfies the duty is L6.
- Section 3 (Terrorist content removal orders): Regulation (EU) 2021/784 art. 3(3) (one hour), art. 3(2) (12 hours’ notice before a provider’s first order, except emergencies), art. 6 (preservation for six months), art. 11 (informing the content provider unless the authority decides otherwise), art. 15(1) and (2) (contact point, electronic receipt, languages). Checked on EUR-Lex 16 Oct 2026 per the CTO pack.
- Section 3 (Terrorist content removal orders): Report form path and on-call rota: S1-E10-02 and S1-E10-09 (decided 16 Oct 2026, 22:59). The board says “Removal order from an authority”; S1-E10-02 calls the path “I’m a competent authority sending a removal order”. Use one label on the form and here.
- Section 3 (Terrorist content removal orders): Which Romanian authority issues removal orders is L6.
- Section 4 (Your personal data): GDPR art. 13(1)(a) and (b), art. 12(2) (make rights easy to use) and 12(3).
- Section 5 (Report illegal content): DSA art. 16(1) to (5) (notice elements, name and email optional for child sexual abuse offences, confirmation, decision). Form: S1-E10-02. Country safety contacts: S1-E10-13.
- Section 6 (Company details): The board shows these as a separate block with five fields (name, registration number, tax ID, address, email). Law 365/2002 art. 5 (as amended by Law 50/2024) asks for easy, direct and permanent access to name, seat, phone, email, trade register number, tax code and further items, and to keep them up to date (research A2). The board has no phone field: please add one.
- Section 6 (Company details): Share capital, EUID and the supervisory authority line are suggestions to check against art. 5 and the Companies Law (Law 31/1990) rules on what must appear on company communications; not researched in the files.
- Section 6 (Company details): SRL or AB is undecided (Q55); SRL proposed for about 1 February 2027 (CTO pack change log).
