# Community guidelines

**Draft for lawyer review, 17 October 2026**

> This is a first draft for a Romanian lawyer (avocat) to review before launch. It is not final and it is not legal advice.

## In short

- Be kind: talk about the writing, not the writer.
- Keep people safe, especially anyone under 18.
- Post your own work, rate it honestly and label fan fiction, parody and AI help.
- Report what’s wrong. A person reads every report.
- Break the rules and we may remove things or limit your account. We always tell you why, and you can ask us to look again.

## 1. Be kind

**In short** (as on the board)

- Comment on the writing, not the writer.
- No bullying, threats or hate.

**Full text**

These guidelines apply everywhere on Ghemotoc: stories, chapters, comments, forums, spaces, profiles, usernames, pictures and private messages.

Talk about the writing, not the writer. Honest feedback helps writers grow. Say what worked and what didn’t, and be specific. Don’t attack the person.

Not allowed:

- bullying, harassment, insults or piling on someone;
- threats of any kind, including “jokes” that sound like threats;
- hate: attacking or demeaning people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, sexual orientation, age or any similar trait;
- spam: the same message again and again, unwanted ads or links posted only to bring people somewhere else;
- usernames or pen names with swearwords, or that pretend to be our staff or Ghemotoc.

Stories are different from comments. A story can show a bully, a villain or a hateful character. That’s fiction. What isn’t allowed is using a story, comment or message to attack real people or groups.

## 2. Keep people safe

**In short** (as on the board)

- No sexual content involving anyone under 18. We remove it straight away.
- Don’t share anyone’s address, phone number, school or photos without their permission.
- Adults can never message anyone under 16. If you’re 16 or 17, messages from adults are off unless you turn them on.

**Full text**

Many people on Ghemotoc are under 18. Keeping them safe comes first.

Never allowed, and reported to the authorities where the law says so:

- sexual content involving anyone under 18, real or fictional, in words or pictures. Stories may tell of abuse, coercion and what they do to people, and characters aged 16 or 17 can have relationships, feelings and kisses, and the story can say that something happened between them. But no story may describe sexual activity involving anyone under 18 in an explicit or eroticised way. Stories about abuse or coercion, or that say something sexual happened, must be rated 16+ or 18+ and carry a content note. A relationship between characters aged 16 or 17 with no sexual content can be rated 13+. We remove anything that crosses this line straight away.
- an adult trying to build a private relationship with someone under 18: for example by asking for pictures, personal details or secrecy or by asking them to move to another app;
- content that supports or praises terrorism or violent extremist groups;
- real threats to hurt someone, or plans to do so.

Pictures. No nude or sexual pictures anywhere on Ghemotoc. Every picture is checked when it’s uploaded, and a flagged picture waits for a moderator. [Founder to confirm: no nude or sexual pictures even in 18+ stories]

Private information. Don’t share anyone’s address, phone number, email, school, real name or photos without their permission. That includes your own if you’re under 18: keep it off your profile and out of public posts.

Messages. Adults can never message anyone under 16. If you’re 16 or 17, messages from adults are off unless you turn them on, and only you can turn them on. Nobody on Ghemotoc can see how old you are, so when someone isn’t taking messages, it just says that.

Spaces. Spaces made by under-16s are only for under-16s. Spaces made by people 16 or over are only for people 16 or over. Class spaces are the one exception: they’re by invitation, and the teacher and pupils of any age are there together.

Self-harm and suicide. Stories can explore hard things like grief, violence or self-harm, if they’re rated right and handled with care. But nothing may encourage or show how to hurt yourself, take your life or stop eating. If someone tells you they’re in danger, report it and we’ll help.

Pretending to be someone else. Don’t pretend to be another person, a teacher, our staff or an organisation.

Scams and illegal things. Don’t try to get money or passwords from people, and don’t sell or promote anything illegal, like drugs or weapons.

## 3. Post your own work

**In short** (as on the board)

- Don’t copy other people’s writing.
- Fan fiction and parody are fine when you label them.
- Rate every story All ages, 13+, 16+ or 18+. Nobody sees a story rated above their age.

**Full text**

Post your own work. Don’t copy other people’s writing, pictures or ideas and pass them off as yours. Short quotes are fine if you name the author and the source.

Fan fiction and parody are welcome. Label them when you publish, and don’t pretend they’re official.

Copied from a published work. If a story copies a published book, readers can report it with the “Copied from a published work” reason. If we confirm it, we add a note to the story or remove it.

Ratings. Rate every story with the rating that fits its strongest part. Nobody sees a story rated above their age.

- All ages: fine for anyone 13 and over and for reading in class. No sexual content or strong swearing. Any violence is mild and not described in detail.
- 13+: can include some swearing, fights and peril, romance without sexual detail and sad or scary themes.
- 16+: can include strong swearing, more intense violence, sex mentioned but not described and drugs or alcohol. Darker themes like self-harm are fine if handled with care.
- 18+: can include sexual content between adults and graphic violence. Only readers with an “18 or over” age check can see it.

If you’re not sure, choose the higher rating. If we think a rating is too low, we may raise it and tell you why. We never lower a rating you chose.

AI. Say whether AI helped: “No AI”, “AI for brainstorming or outlining” or “Partly AI-written”. If several reports that a story hides AI writing are confirmed, it gets an “AI-written, not disclosed” label.

## 4. If you see something wrong

**In short** (as on the board)

- Use the Report button. A person reads every report.
- No account? Use the public report form.

**Full text**

Use the Report button. Every story, chapter, comment, profile, space, thread, post, picture and message has one. A person on our team reads every report. We confirm we got it, and we tell you what we decided.

No account? Use the public report form. It takes every kind of report, including copyright complaints and reports about a child’s safety.

Block. You can block anyone from their profile or from a conversation. They can’t message, mention or follow you, and they aren’t told.

If someone is in danger right now, call your local emergency number first (112 in Romania and across the EU). The report form shows the emergency number, the children’s helpline and where to report child abuse images for your country.

Please report honestly. Reporting the same person again and again without a reason is itself against the rules.

## 5. If you break the rules

**In short** (as on the board)

- We may remove the content, give you a warning or limit your account.
- We always tell you why, and you can ask us to look again.

**Full text**

What we may do depends on how serious it is and whether it has happened before:

- take no action, if the content is fine;
- remove the content, or cut out the exact part that breaks the rules;
- hide a story or limit who can see it, or raise its rating;
- add a label or a note;
- give you a warning;
- suspend your account for a time: you can still sign in, read and download your data, but you can’t post, comment or message;
- close your account. You can’t sign up again with the same email address.

For the most serious things, like sexual content involving a child or real threats, we close the account straight away and may tell the police.

If you keep sending reports that turn out to be groundless, we may pause your reporting for a while.

We always tell you what we did and why, and whether any automatic tool was involved. You can ask us to look again within six months. Where possible, a different moderator decides. See our Notice and action policy for the details.
