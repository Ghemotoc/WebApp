## Everything decided on 16 Oct 2026 (all settled; reflect these)
1. **Positioning:** privacy and owning your writing come first. "Write stories. Keep your rights." Everyone aged 13+ is welcome; students are the *first* community, not the only audience. Don't frame the product as a school or student tool.
2. **Markets:** launch in English and Romanian. **All English-speaking countries open at the same time as Romania** (UK, Ireland, US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore and others), not one at a time. A language opens only with moderators who read it; the founding team moderates in English and Romanian from day one. Writers pick each story's language.
3. **Australia:** under-16 social media minimum age law in force since 10 Dec 2025 likely applies, so Australia is effectively 16+ unless a lawyer says otherwise (OPEN). UK Online Safety Act duties and US child-privacy law apply from launch. Legal check per country before the March 2027 test.
4. **March 2027 target:** 20 writers publishing (from any open market), 200 readers, 30% coming back for the next chapter. Not "20 Romanian student writers".
5. **Sign-in:** email and password (with confirm password) first, then Google, Apple, Microsoft and passkeys. Yoti age check still applies to everyone; minimum age 13; parental consent under 16.
6. **Country:** read from the connection (country code only, no IP stored) is a PROPOSAL, not a decision.
7. **Web and app:** writers choose per story "web and app" or "app only" (more privacy, harder to copy, never "can't be copied"). Apps let readers use any device font; the web uses our fonts.
8. **Embeds:** pictures (checked first), drawings, Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Vimeo, Loom (click to load).
9. **Sharing:** share a passage or a picture like on Substack: link to the exact spot, quote card, picture card. Quote cards can use a brand colour, the story cover or the writer's own picture (blurred).
10. **Covers:** drawn covers from 16 backgrounds (9 plain colours: plum, ink, violet, gold, lilac, blush, sky, sage, paper; 7 patterns: gold stripes, polka dots, grid paper, waves, starry night, doodles, two-tone), recolourable from the palette; or upload (best 1600 × 2560 px); Canva integration is an idea for later.
11. **Messaging:** adults can't message under-18s by default; under-16s can't message adults at all, not even teachers. New accounts can start 10 new chats a day in their first 7 days.
12. **Class spaces:** private spaces for a school class, made by teachers with a Teacher badge; for March, staff set them up for pilot schools. Certificates co-signed by schools.
13. **Money (supporter tier):** comes **later**, once a writer monetisation programme exists. Pay comes only from paying supporters, never ads. Formula B (user-centric): each supporter's writer share goes to the writers that supporter read. Example: of €5 a month, €2 to writers, €3 stays with the company (basis after VAT and store fees still OPEN). Only writers 18+, in a country we can pay out to, with 1,000+ followers are paid. Monthly report a month later; writers withdraw any time through a licensed payout provider.
14. **Website:** its own database, separate from the app (a fork of the existing CMS). Home page is reader-and-writer first, not B2B.
15. **Home and Discover:** separate screens for now, Discover is the main one; they merge into one dashboard later.
16. **Brand line:** "An app by The Magic Coffin" (not "A house of brands by The Magic Coffin") wherever the app is signed off.
17. **Doodles:** a hand-drawn doodle set (fine ink with soft colour) will decorate the website, empty states and certificates.
18. **Not doing now:** the 2027 county school-contest calendars; the decks' Romanian versions come after this refresh.
