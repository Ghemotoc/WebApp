## Method: objective-research-agent (mandatory)
1. Decompose your sub-topic into its parts and list them at the top of the report (taxonomy).
2. Research with WebSearch and WebFetch. Search in Romanian as well as English. Prefer primary sources: legislatie.just.ro, Monitorul Oficial, ONRC (onrc.ro), ANAF (anaf.ro), Inspecția Muncii (inspectiamuncii.ro), ANSPDCP, ANCOM, ANPC, OSIM, BNR, Ministerul Economiei/AIMMAIPE, ADR Sud-Muntenia, MIPE/fonduri-ue, EUR-Lex, European Commission, official programme sites, Swedish authorities (Skatteverket, Försäkringskassan, Bolagsverket, Vinnova, Almi) where relevant. Use reputable secondary sources (Big Four, law firms, StartupCafe, Ziarul Financiar, Juridice.ro, Wall-Street.ro) to locate and explain, but anchor claims in primary text where possible.
3. Currency matters: Romanian tax and labour rules changed a lot in 2025 and 2026. Always state the rule "as in force on [date]" and give the legal act (law/OUG/HG/Order number). Flag anything announced but not yet in force, and anything you could not confirm is current.
4. Failed Access Log: record every URL that fails to load (URL, tool, error). Then retry it with Claude in Chrome: load the tools with ToolSearch query "select:mcp__claude-in-chrome__tabs_context_mcp,mcp__claude-in-chrome__tabs_create_mcp,mcp__claude-in-chrome__navigate,mcp__claude-in-chrome__get_page_text,mcp__claude-in-chrome__tabs_close_mcp", open your OWN new tab, read the page, close your tab. Read-only: never log in, fill forms, click consent beyond declining non-essential cookies, or download files. If Chrome is unavailable, say so in the log.
5. Per-source record for EVERY item you read: Source (APA 7 with URL), Summary (what it says), Significance (what it means for a business like this), Cross-Source Relation (how it fits the other sources).
6. Per-section narrative with APA in-text citations, covering only: "What is the problem?" and "What exists?". STRICTLY DIAGNOSTIC: no recommendations, no "you should", no action plans. Facts such as deadlines, rates, costs, thresholds, eligibility rules and procedures ARE "what exists" and must be included precisely.
7. "Section References" block at the end of every section: full APA 7 with clickable URLs.
8. Dated items: a table "Calendar items" (Date or recurrence | What | Applies to | Legal basis | Source URL) with every deadline, recurring filing date, programme window or effective date you find.
9. "Key links" table: the official portals and pages someone running this business would bookmark (Name | What it's for | URL).
10. Master Reference List at the end, then a verification pass: every URL must be one you actually retrieved or saw in search results in this run. Remove or flag any you cannot match. Zero invented URLs.
