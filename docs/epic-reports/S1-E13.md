## What works

- Under-18s start on the most private value of every privacy choice, and the database fills the defaults in, so a screen can't get them wrong.
- A person can download their data and their stories whatever their account state: read-only while a parent hasn't agreed, suspended or waiting for an age check. Only a closing account can't, until it is kept.
- Nothing about a person leaves their own browser except the finished file, which only they can download, for seven days. The EPUB isn't stored at all.
- The two lists, what goes in the data file and what is deleted with the account, name the same tables, and a new user table fails tests `210`, `211` and `214` until it is in both.
- Every keep time is a setting, and the nightly run records how much each clean-up removed, never which rows.

## Known gaps

- **A closing account can't download its data.** During the 14 days the closing page stands in front of everything, and `my_data_export()` refuses a closed profile; the person can log in and choose "Keep my account", download, and close it again. IDEA-20261101-2 ("Download your stories first" before deleting) would offer the download on the confirm step instead.
- **The data file shows a privacy choice nobody made as empty.** `profiles.profile_visible_to` is null until the person picks one, meaning their age group's default (people they follow under 18, everyone for adults); `data.json` shows the stored null, not the default. IDEA-20261101-1 (a "What's in this file" guide) is the place to explain it.
- **Files of deleted rows stay in Storage until the sweep is set up** on staging and production (`docs/mocks.md`, GO-LIVE).
- **No PDF** of stories yet: Word and EPUB only (S1-E11-09b).
