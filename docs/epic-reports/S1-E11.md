## What works

- The server decides, not the menu: every staff page reads and writes only through `admin_*` functions and policies that ask `staff_may()` → `is_staff_session()`; the menu is built from the same answer.
- A staff session ends after 12 hours and a role change needs a sign-in from the last 15 minutes, both enforced in the database.
- Invite links admit exactly the number of people they say, the last place goes once under a race, and nothing records who used a link.
- The audit log is append-only, searchable by action, staff member, account and dates, exportable as CSV, with the export itself logged; usernames and an 8-character target ID, never a full user ID.
