## What works

- An invited adult can like a chapter, comment and reply, react, downvote privately and follow writers and stories; each of these is one action and undoable.
- Counts are public where the boards show them (likes, reactions, followers) and never say who; downvotes have no count for anyone but the moderation queue.
- Every write checks the account gate, so the minors' accounts read but can't write until their gate opens (S1-E2-08, S1-E2-03).
- Ratings and blocks are checked once, in `readable_chapter()`, so comments, reactions, likes and downvotes all follow the chapter: a reader who can't open the chapter can't reach anything on it.
- Blocking ends follows both ways and hides each side's comments from the other.

## Breaks found where stories meet

None. The journeys passed as written; the only changes were to the tests' own expectations (an unreadable chapter's comments answer null rather than an error, which is the comments story's documented behaviour).

## Known gaps

- A notification for a comment opens the chapter, not the comment itself: comments load after the chapter text, so a link can't jump to one yet (follow-up).
- Forum downvotes come with the forums (S1-E8, after March).
- The comment limit per minute is a fixed setting; rate limits per account are S1-E10-12 (after March).
