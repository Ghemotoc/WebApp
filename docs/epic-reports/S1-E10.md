## What works

- Any signed-in account reports, read-only ones included; the reported account never learns who. The same item reported again within a day gives the same reference; the hourly limit holds.
- Every rule is in the database: the case per item, the tiers, the deadline from the first report, who sees a safeguarding case, what a decision does, the account gate's `suspended` and `banned`, the banned email at sign-up, the reporting pause. The screens only explain.
- Staff see usernames and age groups, never a year of birth; members never see a user ID.
- Every staff action and every lift, pause and restore has a reason and an audit row.
- The helplines show for the reader's own country, with the official links and the check date, and never a number typed from memory.
