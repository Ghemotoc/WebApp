## What works

- A signed-in reader reads a whole chapter as real text, moves between chapters, sees how far they are and keeps their place by selecting text. Copying is harder, as the notice honestly says.
- A shared link works before it is opened:
  - every app shows the title, pen name and blurb, from tags written on the server;
  - search engines index story pages only.
- After it is opened, a signed-out visitor gets the first part of the chapter and a prompt. The rest never reaches their browser.
- Ratings hold across all three stories: under-16s and visitors never get a 16+ chapter, its teaser or its blurb, and 18+ needs an adult.
- Reads are daily totals per chapter, counted once per tab, never the writer's own and never who read.
- The reader stays within its budgets on a throttled phone (about 1.5 s to the first paragraph; 12 KB of its own script) and reflows at 320 px and at 200% zoom.
