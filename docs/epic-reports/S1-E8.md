## What works

- One account gate holds minors back: while `adults_only` is on, an under-18 reads every forum, space and class they may see but can't post; once it is off, kid_14_waiting still waits for a parent and kid_14_ok posts.
- A class is one more space with a hidden forum, so threads, quotes, read marks, locks, search, signatures and ranks work in it with no extra code.
- Age groups never mix where people talk: an under-16 can't join a 16+ space, an under-16 space is hidden from adults until its owner opens it, and a class is seen only by its members and staff.
- No screen, link or function result shows a user ID; quotes, posts, ranks and certificates name people by handle and pen name.
- A certificate check never shows the student, and a real name printed on a certificate lives only there, deleted 30 days after the class is archived.

## Breaks found where stories meet

No break in behaviour was found. Two test-side fixes rode in part e (#281): the clean-up of `certificates.spec.ts` deleted from the append-only audit log (now left alone), and the retention test counted seven clean-ups instead of eight.

## Known gaps

- **Contest certificates** wait for contests (S1-E12-02).
- **The certificate PDF** is the browser's print of the page (Q49 is OPEN); a server-made PDF could follow with S1-E11-09b.
- **The check page has no rate limit** of its own; S1-E10-12 could add one.
- **Forums refresh by reloading**: new posts appear on the next load or "New for you", not live (no Realtime in the epic).
- **No link to My certificates** from the account menu yet; the notification and the certificate pages link there.
