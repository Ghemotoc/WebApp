## What works

- The people who should hear about something do, within a minute, and never about their own action.
- What a person is shown is decided when they look, with their own rules: a chapter rated above their age, a moderated item or someone either side blocks is left out, so the list and the badge agree.
- Grouped rows say "a, b and 10 others" and move to the top, unread again, when someone new joins them.
- A moderation decision always arrives and says "Always on" (DSA art. 17); the statement of reasons joins it with S1-E10-04.
- Notifications are kept 90 days (a decision a year) and purged nightly (`docs/retention.md`).

## Breaks found where stories meet

| Found | Stories | What happened | Fixed |

| Deleting a case failed once notifications pointed at it | S1-E10 tests × S1-E7-01 | The new `case_id` reference had no `on delete` | In this bundle: `on delete cascade` |

None are open.

## Known gaps

- The bell is refreshed every minute and on focus, not pushed (decision in the pull request); Realtime can replace it without changing the table.
- No notification settings: every kind is on, in the app only (S1-E7-02, after March).
- A comment notification opens the chapter, not the comment (comments load after the chapter).
- The reporter isn't told the outcome of their report yet, and a decision has no statement of reasons or appeal link yet (S1-E10-04, S1-E10-07 in SAFE-3).
