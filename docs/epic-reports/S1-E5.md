## What works

- Every list is the same function with the same reader rules: ratings (rule 18), blocks either way, moderation and drafts.
- Popular is the same for everyone: no personal data and nothing from what one reader read (Stage 1 has no personalised feed).
- Staff can change the shelf order, the weights and how new a "rising" writer is with a setting; the next page load or hourly run uses it.
- The story page shows likes, comments, chapters and the last update; reads stay the writer's own.

## Known gaps

- Shares count 0 in the score until sharing exists (S1-E4-05, `docs/mocks.md`).
- The score is recomputed every hour, so a like moves a story on the next run, not at once.
- A writer's profile shows "View more" only past 20 stories (rule 20); below that the shelf is the whole list.
