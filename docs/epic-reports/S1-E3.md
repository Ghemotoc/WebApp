## What works

- A writer can go from an empty form to a published, scheduled and reordered story, and every step is checked again in the database. The screens only explain.
- Ratings hide stories from younger readers everywhere a reader can reach a story today (`get_story_page()`); a signed-out visitor counts as under 16.
- The editor never loses words:
  - it autosaves every 10 seconds and on blur;
  - it keeps a copy on the device while offline;
  - a version check stops one device overwriting another;
  - text pasted from Word and Google Docs is cleaned.
- Chapter text is a Markdown subset, checked twice (zod and `check_chapter_text()`) and drawn by one safe renderer (`ChapterText`).
- Publishing, scheduling (Romania time, both clock-change nights tested) and unpublishing all go through one internal function, so readers, jobs and the story's dates always agree.
- Deleting a story or a chapter hides it at once, allows undo for 30 days, and nightly jobs purge what is left.
- Every screen in the epic passes axe, fits 390 px and keeps its doodles off text and controls.

## What Robert does by hand on staging

Once the epic's PRs are merged in order, the migrations reach staging through Supabase's GitHub integration. Then:

1. Run `supabase/staging-topups/S1-E3-01.sql`. It adds the three seed stories and their tags (`docs/runbooks/staging-reset-and-seed.md`, "Top-ups").
2. Run `supabase/staging-topups/S1-E3-06.sql`, after the S1-E3-06 migration. It sets the third seed story to 13+. Both top-ups are safe to run twice.
3. Check the three jobs with `select jobname, schedule from cron.job;`:
   - `publish-due-chapters` runs every minute;
   - `purge-deleted-stories` runs at 03:23;
   - `purge-deleted-chapters` runs at 03:29.
4. Walk journey 1 above as `adult_writer` at phone width, then open the story signed out.
5. Read every new Romanian line in each PR's screens yourself (decided 17 Oct 2026, 19:33).
