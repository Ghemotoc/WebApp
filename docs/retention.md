# Retention schedule

How long each kind of personal data is kept and what removes it (architecture 1.10: "Retention schedule in `docs/retention.md`, enforced by `pg_cron` jobs"). Started by S1-E1-02; every story that stores a new kind of personal data adds its row in the same pull request (Definition of Done, Privacy).

Rows marked **proposal** wait for Robert and the lawyer (docs/for-the-lawyer.md). The data protection impact assessment and the record of processing come with the legal stories (S1-E14-01).

| Data | Where | Kept for | Removed by | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Year of birth, and the month only when the year leaves the age unclear at 13 or 16 | `profiles.birth_year`, `profiles.birth_month` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05); the 14-day clean-up when no age check passes (S1-E2-08) | architecture 1.10; S1-E2-02 |
| Birthday, optional: the month, and the day if the person adds it, never the year | `profiles.birthday_month`, `profiles.birthday_day` | until the person removes it in Settings, or the life of the account | the person (Settings › Birthday); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-12; Robert, 25 Oct 2026, 07:08 to 07:11 |
| Age check result, date and method | `profiles.age_check_result`, `age_checked_at`, `age_check_method` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | architecture 1.10; S1-E2-08 |
| A Google, Apple or Microsoft account linked to the person's: the provider's own ID for them and what it sent (the email and, where given, a name); never shown on any screen | `auth.identities` (Supabase Auth) | until the person disconnects it in Settings › Ways to log in, or the life of the account | the person (Settings › Ways to log in); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-09 |
| A provider sign-up that was never finished: an account with no profile | `auth.users`, `auth.identities` | at most a day | cancelled at once when a sign-up rule refuses; otherwise the pg_cron job `purge-unfinished-social-sign-ups` (at 17 minutes past every hour) | S1-E2-09 |
| A passkey: its public key, its name, when it was added and last used; never the fingerprint or face, which stay on the device | `auth.webauthn_credentials` (Supabase Auth) | until the person removes it in Settings › Ways to log in, or the life of the account | the person (Settings › Ways to log in); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-09 |
| That the person asked to connect a provider from Settings | `identity_link_requests` | 10 minutes; removed as soon as the provider joins | used up by `guard_identity_link()`; expired rows are ignored and replaced by the next request; account deletion (cascade) | S1-E2-09 |
| Each finished Yoti check: the line (13 or 18), passed or failed, the method and the date; never a photo or a document | `age_check_attempts` | 6 months | the pg_cron job `purge-old-age-check-attempts` (03:23 UTC every night); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-08b |
| A request for a manual age check: when it was asked, the outcome and the reason the person reads (who decided is only in the audit log) | `manual_age_checks` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-08b |
| A county, only if the person picks one | `profiles.region_code` | until the person clears it in Settings, or the life of the account | the person (Settings › What you like); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-06 |
| What the person likes: categories to read and write and topics, as their own explicit choices; never inferred from reading | `reading_preferences` | until the person changes them in Settings, or the life of the account | the person (Settings › What you like); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-06 |
| Whether a 16 or 17-year-old lets adults message them (off until they turn it on) | `profiles.adults_may_message` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-06; S1-E9-03 |
| Who may message the person: everyone, people they follow or nobody (empty means the default for their age group) | `profiles.messages_from` | the life of the account | the person (Settings, S1-E9-04); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E9-03 |
| Private chats: who started each, who it went to, whether it is a request, accepted or declined, and when each person last read it | `conversations`, `conversation_members` | until both people delete the chat | the clean-up job (S1-E9-04); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E9-01 |
| Private messages: the words and when they were sent | `messages` | until both people delete the chat; a reported one and the 20 before it (its context, S1-E9-02) as long as their case needs | the clean-up job (S1-E9-04); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E9-01 |
| A parent's or guardian's consent: their email, the email's language, a hash of the link's token, the status (waiting, agreed, refused, withdrawn, replaced), whether the guardian box was ticked, the privacy notice and terms versions and the dates | `parental_consents` | the life of the account; a refusal or an unanswered request after 14 days erases the account with it | `erase_unchecked_account()`; account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-03; GDPR art. 8 |
| A Teacher badge request: the way (school email or proof), the school address's domain only (never the address), a hash of the link's token, the status, the reason the person reads and the dates | `teacher_checks` | the life of the account (a replaced or decided request stays so the person sees the reason); who decided is only in the audit log | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-11 |
| A proof that someone teaches (a letter or a staff card; a picture loses its location data first) | Storage bucket `teacher-proofs` | until staff decide: the file is deleted before the decision is saved | `admin_decide_teacher_check()` refuses while the file is there; a file left by an unfinished upload is a follow-up | S1-E2-11 |
| When the person got the Teacher badge | `profiles.teacher_badge_since` | until staff remove the badge, or the life of the account | `admin_remove_teacher_badge()`; account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-11 |
| An account whose age check isn't done 14 days after sign-up (and no manual check is open): its year and month of birth, pen name, bio and birthday, and its email and login | `profiles`, `auth.users` | 14 days from sign-up | the pg_cron job `erase-unchecked-accounts` (02:45 UTC every night): the profile is marked deleted, those fields are erased, the email becomes `<id>@deleted.invalid` and the login is shut; the row itself goes with account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-08b |
| Username and pen name, and when each last changed | `profiles.handle`, `handle_slug`, `handle_skeleton`, `handle_changed_at`, `pen_name`, `pen_name_changed_at` | the life of the account; a deleted account's username stays reserved (only its folded forms) so nobody can take it to fake the person (**proposal**) | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-04 |
| Bio (up to 300 characters, public on the profile) and the online-status opt-in (off) | `profiles.bio`, `profiles.shows_online_status` | the life of the account; the person can empty the bio at any time | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E5-05 |
| Who blocks whom (no reason, no text) | `blocks` | until the person unblocks, or either account is deleted | `set_block()` (unblock); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E5-05 |
| Who follows which writer and which story | `follows_users`, `follows_stories` | until the person unfollows, a block ends it, the story is deleted for good, or either account is deleted | `follow_writer()` / `follow_story()` (unfollow); `set_block()`; the story purge (a cascade); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E6-05 |
| What a reader keeps in their library and how far they got in each chapter, with the kind of screen (phone, tablet or computer, never a device name) | `library_entries`, `reading_positions` | until the person takes the story off their shelves (the places stay so they can carry on later), the story is deleted for good, or the account is deleted (**proposal**: places older than 2 years could be purged) | `set_library_shelf()`; the story purge (a cascade); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E4-03 |
| A reader's reading settings: font, text size, line spacing, line width and theme | `reading_settings` | while the account exists; the reader changes them at any time | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E4-02 |
| Which chapters a reader made a quote card from (never the passage or the picture, which stay on the device) | `quote_cards` | while the account exists, so the limit of one per chapter holds; gone with the chapter (a cascade) | the chapter purge; account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E4-07 |
| Earlier usernames and pen names | `audit_log` (`profile.handle_changed`, `profile.pen_name_changed`) | as the audit log, 3 years | as the audit log | S1-E2-04; architecture 1.6 |
| A moderator's approval of a name refused by mistake (the name, the reason, who approved it) | `username_approvals` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-04 |
| A notice or removal order from the public form (the address, the explanation, the notifier's name and email, an authority's name and reference; no IP address) | `public_notices`, `on_call_alerts` | 3 years after the notice is closed (**proposal**, setting `public_notices_keep_years`), as the form's "Your details" says | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-old-public-notices` (`purge_old_public_notices()`) | S1-E10-02 |
| A copied-work report's source and passages, and a moderator's question to a reporter with the answer (the reporter's account, the case, the question and answer text) | `reports` (the `copy_source_*` and `reported_passage` columns), `report_detail_requests` | as long as the report and its case; **OPEN** with the reports' own retention | the reports' purge, once a retention rule for reports and cases is set (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-08 |
| A public note on a story ("parts of this story copy a published work"; the story, the writer and the case) | `copied_work_notes` | while the story exists, or until an appeal removes it (S1-E10-07) | deleted with the story | S1-E10-08 |
| A statement of reasons (the action, the facts in staff's words, the rule, the item's summary, the person's account, the case) | `statements_of_reasons` | 6 months after the decision, the time to challenge it, then as long as its case (**OPEN** with the reports' own retention); the person reads it on /my-reports | the reports' and cases' purge, once their retention rule is set (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-04; DSA art. 17 and art. 20(1) |
| An item taken down under a removal order (the item itself, its case and its timestamps) | the item's own table, held by `cases.preserve_until` | 6 months from the take-down (setting `removal_order_preserve_months`), longer only if the authority asks in writing; the content purges skip it until then | the usual purge jobs, once `preserve_until` has passed (`content_is_held_for_moderation()`) | S1-E10-09; Regulation (EU) 2021/784 art. 6 |
| Who is on call for removal orders, and when | `on_call_rota` | 1 year after the shift ends (**proposal**) | a purge job in S1-E13-04; until then nothing is deleted | S1-E10-09 |
| A referral to an authority on a safeguarding case (the authority, their reference, a note, who referred) | `safeguarding_referrals` | as long as its case (**OPEN** with the reports' own retention) | the cases' purge (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-10; DSA art. 18 |
| An evidence pack (the content, the account's details and email, the reports and timestamps) | never stored by us: built on request and downloaded by the staff member, logged as a sensitive read | as long as the referral needs it, on the staff member's device; the safeguarding runbook says to delete it after handing it over | the staff member (docs/runbooks/safeguarding.md) | S1-E10-10 |

| A chapter's unsaved title and text on the writer's own device (no user ID) | the browser's IndexedDB (`writing-drafts`, one record per chapter code) | only while something is unsaved: removed as soon as the server has the text, or when the writer keeps the other device's version | the editor itself (S1-E3-02b); clearing the browser | S1-E3-02b |
| A story or chapter the writer deleted (title, text, settings) | `stories`, `chapters`, `chapter_revisions` with `deleted_at` set | 30 days from the deletion (setting `content_purge_days`), hidden from readers at once; the writer can undo until then.
