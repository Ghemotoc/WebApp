# Retention schedule

How long each kind of personal data is kept and what removes it (architecture 1.10: "Retention schedule in `docs/retention.md`, enforced by `pg_cron` jobs"). Started by S1-E1-02; every story that stores a new kind of personal data adds its row in the same pull request (Definition of Done, Privacy).

Rows marked **proposal** wait for Robert and the lawyer (docs/for-the-lawyer.md). The data protection impact assessment and the record of processing come with the legal stories (S1-E14-01).

| Data | Where | Kept for | Removed by | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Year of birth, and the month only when the year leaves the age unclear at 13 or 16 | `profiles.birth_year`, `profiles.birth_month` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05); the 14-day clean-up when no age check passes (S1-E2-08) | architecture 1.10; S1-E2-02 |
| Birthday, optional: the month, and the day if the person adds it, never the year | `profiles.birthday_month`, `profiles.birthday_day` | until the person removes it in Settings, or the life of the account | the person (Settings › Birthday); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-12; Robert, 25 Oct 2026, 07:08 to 07:11 |
| Age check result, date and method | `profiles.age_check_result`, `age_checked_at`, `age_check_method` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | architecture 1.10; S1-E2-08 |
| Username and pen name, and when each last changed | `profiles.handle`, `handle_slug`, `handle_skeleton`, `handle_changed_at`, `pen_name`, `pen_name_changed_at` | the life of the account; a deleted account's username stays reserved (only its folded forms) so nobody can take it to fake the person (**proposal**) | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-04 |
| Bio (up to 300 characters, public on the profile) and the online-status opt-in (off) | `profiles.bio`, `profiles.shows_online_status` | the life of the account; the person can empty the bio at any time | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E5-05 |
| Who blocks whom (no reason, no text) | `blocks` | until the person unblocks, or either account is deleted | `set_block()` (unblock); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E5-05 |
| Who follows which writer and which story | `follows_users`, `follows_stories` | until the person unfollows, a block ends it, the story is deleted for good, or either account is deleted | `follow_writer()` / `follow_story()` (unfollow); `set_block()`; the story purge (a cascade); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E6-05 |
| What a reader keeps in their library and how far they got in each chapter, with the kind of screen (phone, tablet or computer, never a device name) | `library_entries`, `reading_positions` | until the person takes the story off their shelves (the places stay so they can carry on later), the story is deleted for good, or the account is deleted (**proposal**: places older than 2 years could be purged) | `set_library_shelf()`; the story purge (a cascade); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E4-03 |
| A reader's reading settings: font, text size, line spacing, line width and theme | `reading_settings` | while the account exists; the reader changes them at any time | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E4-02 |
| Which chapters a reader made a quote card from (never the passage or the picture, which stay on the device) | `quote_cards` | while the account exists, so the limit of one per chapter holds; gone with the chapter (a cascade) | the chapter purge; account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E4-07 |
| Earlier usernames and pen names | `audit_log` (`profile.handle_changed`, `profile.pen_name_changed`) | as the audit log, 3 years | as the audit log | S1-E2-04; architecture 1.6 |
| A moderator's approval of a name refused by mistake (the name, the reason, who approved it) | `username_approvals` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-04 |
| A notice or removal order from the public form (the address, the explanation, the notifier's name and email, an authority's name and reference; no IP address) | `public_notices`, `on_call_alerts` | 3 years after the notice is closed (**proposal**, setting `public_notices_keep_years`), as the form's "Your details" says | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-old-public-notices` (`purge_old_public_notices()`) | S1-E10-02 |
| A copied-work report's source and passages, and a moderator's question to a reporter with the answer (the reporter's account, the case, the question and answer text) | `reports` (the `copy_source_*` and `reported_passage` columns), `report_detail_requests` | as long as the report and its case; **OPEN** with the reports' own retention | the reports' purge, once a retention rule for reports and cases is set (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-08 |
| A public note on a story ("parts of this story copy a published work"; the story, the writer and the case) | `copied_work_notes` | while the story exists, or until an appeal removes it (S1-E10-07) | deleted with the story | S1-E10-08 |
| A statement of reasons (the action, the facts in staff's words, the rule, the item's summary, the person's account, the case) | `statements_of_reasons` | 6 months after the decision, the time to challenge it, then as long as its case (**OPEN** with the reports' own retention); the person reads it on /my-reports | the reports' and cases' purge, once their retention rule is set (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-04; DSA art. 17 and art. 20(1) |
| An item taken down under a removal order (the item itself, its case and its timestamps) | the item's own table, held by `cases.preserve_until` | 6 months from the take-down (setting `removal_order_preserve_months`), longer only if the authority asks in writing; the content purges skip it until then | the usual purge jobs, once `preserve_until` has passed (`content_is_held_for_moderation()`) | S1-E10-09; Regulation (EU) 2021/784 art. 6 |
| Who is on call for removal orders, and when | `on_call_rota` | 1 year after the shift ends (**proposal**) | a purge job in S1-E13-04; until then nothing is deleted | S1-E10-09 |
| A referral to an authority on a safeguarding case (the authority, their reference, a note, who referred) | `safeguarding_referrals` | as long as its case (**OPEN** with the reports' own retention) | the cases' purge (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-10; DSA art. 18 |
| An evidence pack (the content, the account's details and email, the reports and timestamps) | never stored by us: built on request and downloaded by the staff member, logged as a sensitive read | as long as the referral needs it, on the staff member's device; the safeguarding runbook says to delete it after handing it over | the staff member (docs/runbooks/safeguarding.md) | S1-E10-10 |
| Country of the connection at sign-up (two-letter code only; the IP address is never stored by us) | `profiles.country_code` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E2-10; decided 16 Oct 2026, Q20 |
| Interface language (ro or en) | `profiles.locale` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E1-04 |
| Interface language and light or night mode on a device | the browser's localStorage (`interface-language`, `theme-preference`) | until the person clears their browser; never sent to us | the person's browser | S1-E1-03, S1-E1-04 |
| A chapter's unsaved title and text on the writer's own device (no user ID) | the browser's IndexedDB (`writing-drafts`, one record per chapter code) | only while something is unsaved: removed as soon as the server has the text, or when the writer keeps the other device's version | the editor itself (S1-E3-02b); clearing the browser | S1-E3-02b |
| A story or chapter the writer deleted (title, text, settings) | `stories`, `chapters`, `chapter_revisions` with `deleted_at` set | 30 days from the deletion (setting `content_purge_days`), hidden from readers at once; the writer can undo until then. Content under an open report is kept for the moderators | the nightly pg_cron jobs `purge-deleted-stories` (`purge_deleted_stories()`) and `purge-deleted-chapters` (`purge_deleted_chapters()`); `docs/runbooks/scheduled-jobs.md` | S1-E3-05, S1-E3-04 |
| A comment the author or the story's writer deleted (its words, spoiler mark, date) | `comments` with `deleted_at` set | 30 days from the deletion (setting `content_purge_days`), gone from readers at once; kept while replies stand under it or a moderation case is open | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-deleted-comments` (`purge_deleted_comments()`) | S1-E6-01 |
| Who likes which chapter | `chapter_likes` | until the reader takes the like back, or the chapter or the account is deleted | the reader's own toggle; the chapter's deletion (a cascade); the account deletion job (S1-E13-04) | S1-E6-03 |
| Who reacted to which comment, and how | `comment_reactions` | until the reader takes the reaction back, or the comment (after its purge), the chapter or the account is deleted | the reader's own toggle; the comment's deletion (a cascade); the account deletion job (S1-E13-04) | S1-E6-02 |
| Who downvoted which comment (private: only the voter and moderators see it) | `comment_downvotes` | until the reader takes it back, or the comment (after its purge), the chapter or the account is deleted | the reader's own toggle; the comment's deletion (a cascade); the account deletion job (S1-E13-04) | S1-E6-04 |
| A person's notifications: what happened, who did it (their user IDs, shown only as usernames), whether it was read | `notifications` | 90 days from the latest activity (setting `notifications_keep_days`); a moderation decision a year, the statement of reasons is kept apart (S1-E10-04) | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-old-notifications` (`purge_old_notifications()`); a deleted story, chapter or comment takes its notifications with it (a cascade) | S1-E7-01 |
| A person's email choices per kind of notification (Instant, Daily digest or Off) | `notification_email_settings` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E7-02 |
| The key in a person's one-click unsubscribe link (random, no meaning) | `email_unsubscribe_keys` | the life of the account | account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E7-02 |
| Which notifications went by email and when (the recipient's user ID and the notifications' IDs; never the address or the words) | `email_outbox` | 30 days after the email left | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-sent-emails` (`purge_sent_emails()`) | S1-E7-02 |
| How many times each chapter was opened each day (totals only: no user ID, no IP address, nothing about the reader) | `chapter_reads` | for as long as the chapter exists; deleted with it (the chapter purge, or the story's) | the chapter's own deletion (a cascade) | S1-E4-01a |
| The March test numbers: daily totals by market (writers who published, signed-up readers, chapter reads, the return rate; no user ID, nothing about a person) | `daily_metrics` | 3 years (setting `daily_metrics_keep_years`) | the daily pg_cron job `count-daily-metrics` deletes older rows each run | S1-E15-02; architecture 1.7.9 |
| A story's popularity score (one number per story, from totals only) | `story_scores` | while the story exists; recomputed every hour | the story's deletion (a cascade) | S1-E5-03 |
| Which version of the terms and the privacy notice a person accepted, and when | `consents` | the life of the account (proof of consent, GDPR Art 7(1)); how long after deletion is **OPEN** for the lawyer | account deletion (S1-E13-05); nobody can change or delete a row through the API | S1-E14-01; privacy notice, section 5 |
| Invites (who made it, a private note, uses, expiry; the code only as a hash; never who used it) | `invites` | 90 days after the invite expires (**proposal**, S1-E11-17) | a purge job in S1-E13-04; until then nothing is deleted | S1-E2-01, S1-E11-17 |
| Staff roles and permissions | `staff_roles`, `staff_permissions` | while the person is staff | role or permission removal (`admin_remove_role()`, `admin_set_permission()`), each logged | architecture 1.8.3; S1-E2-07a |
| Audit log rows (who acted, on whose account) | `audit_log` | 3 years from the action; never edited by any role | a purge job in the retention jobs story (S1-E13-04, piece B17); until it exists nothing is deleted. The append-only triggers refuse deletes for every role, the owner included, so that job needs one deliberate, audited way to remove rows older than 3 years | decided 17 Oct 2026, 17:41 (Robert); architecture 1.6; S1-E11-01 |
| Wrong log-in tries (a SHA-256 hash of the email and the time; no email, user ID or IP) | `sign_in_failures` | 24 hours | `record_sign_in_failure()` removes older rows each time it runs; a successful log-in or a password reset removes the person's rows at once | S1-E2-05; decided 17 Oct 2026, 19:43, Q12 |
| Signed-in devices (browser user agent, start and last active times) | Supabase's own `auth.sessions` (it also keeps the IP address, which we never read or show) | until the person logs out, logs the device out or the session ends | Supabase Auth | S1-E2-05; Q13 |
| Browser error reports (error name, scrubbed message, route pattern, app version, browser family, time; no user ID, email, IP, user agent or user text) | `client_errors` | 30 days (**proposal**, setting `client_errors_keep_days`) | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-old-client-errors` (`purge_old_client_errors()`) | S1-E1-05; decided 18 Oct 2026, 10:46 (own error log) |
| An uploaded cover or profile picture and its check (the file, who uploaded it, labels and scores from the check, never the picture sent anywhere but the two checking services) | the `covers` and `avatars` buckets, `picture_checks` | while it is the story's cover or the person's picture; a removed one as long as its case (**OPEN** with the reports' own retention). A known child abuse match is preserved 6 months for the authorities (`preserve_until`) | replaced or removed by the person or a moderator; the cases' purge (S1-E13-04); account deletion (S1-E13-05) | S1-E10-05; S1-E10-10 |
| An appeal (the person's explanation, the outcome, who decided, the message sent) | `appeals` | as long as its statement of reasons and case (**OPEN** with the reports' own retention) | the cases' purge (S1-E13-04) | S1-E10-07; DSA art. 20 |
| Messages between a member and the moderation team, with staff's internal notes | `moderator_mail_threads`, `moderator_mail_messages` | 1 year after the thread was last updated (**proposal**) | a purge job in S1-E13-04; until then nothing is deleted | S1-E10-11 |
| Rate-limit events (which action, when, by which account; no content, no IP address) | `rate_limit_events` | 2 days (the limits look back one hour) | the nightly pg_cron job `purge-rate-limit-events` (`purge_rate_limit_events()`) | S1-E10-12 |
| Who confirmed each safety contact and when, and the monthly checks | `safety_contacts.last_checked_by_user_id`, `safety_contact_checks` | while the contact exists; checks 2 years (**proposal**) | a purge job in S1-E13-04; until then nothing is deleted | S1-E10-13b |
| IP addresses | not stored: the rate limits (S1-E10-12) count per account, and Cloudflare's own rate-limit rule handles signed-out traffic before it reaches us | scrambled, kept 30 days, if a later story stores them | that story | decided 16 Oct 2026, Q48 |
| Class certificates with a real name | not stored yet | deleted 30 days after the class is archived | S1-E8-12 | decided 17 Oct 2026, 12:44 |
