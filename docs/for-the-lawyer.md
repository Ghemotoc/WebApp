### Found while building S1-E2-12 and S1-E2-13 (26 Oct 2026)

- **An optional birthday.** A person may add their birthday month, and the day if they like, never the year, so the app can wish them a happy birthday; only they see it and staff can correct it only when asked (the correction is logged). The year of birth from sign-up is stored separately and is never combined with it on any screen or export to staff. Please confirm that a day and month kept apart from the year is not a "full date of birth" under our own rule, that consent is the right basis for it, and that the new line in the privacy notice drafts (section "What you give us") is enough. The line is in `content/legal/` but not yet in a published legal version: it goes live with the next version you approve.
- **The joining anniversary.** In the month someone joined, the signed-in greeting says "Happy anniversary". It uses only the account's creation date, which we already keep. Please confirm no new notice line is needed.

### Found while building S1-E10-08 (24 Oct 2026)

- **The public note on a copied story.** When moderators find that parts of a story copy a published work, they may leave the story up and show every reader "Moderators found that parts of this story copy a published work." instead of removing it. Please confirm this note is not defamatory towards the writer when the finding is wrong, that it needs a statement of reasons to the writer (DSA art. 17, built in S1-E10-04) and that the writer can appeal it (S1-E10-07). Needs the lawyer's review before launch.
- **Asking the reporter for more details.** A moderator can ask the person who reported for more (for example the page in the published work); the reporter answers in Settings. The question and answer are kept with the report. Please confirm this is covered by the purpose already stated for reports in the privacy notice.
