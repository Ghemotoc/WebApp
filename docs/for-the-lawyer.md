### Found while building CLASS-POINTS: class points and teacher notes (13 Nov 2026)

- **A teacher's private note to a pupil.** With their points on a pupil's chapter or assignment reply, a teacher may write a private note. Only that pupil, the teacher and our moderators read it; it has no reply box, so it can't become a private conversation between an adult and a minor; every save is a line in the class's log; the pupil can report it; staff can turn notes off everywhere with one switch (`class_teacher_notes`). Robert decided this is on (13 Nov 2026). Please confirm it sits within the rule that adults can't message under-16s, and whether a parent should be told about it in the consent text.
- **Who gave points.** A pupil's points to a classmate are stored with the giver, but no pupil and no teacher is ever shown who gave them; only moderators, when a flag is open, and that read is logged. Please confirm this suits the privacy notice and the record of processing.
- **Kept for.** Class points, notes and the record of which chapters a pupil opened in their class are deleted 30 days after the class is archived, and at once when the pupil leaves the class (docs/retention.md).
- **Class competitions: the Winner is always announced.** In a class competition pupils choose to join. When the teacher confirms the results, the Winner's pen name and winning chapter, and the "Readers' choice" pen names, are shown to the whole class with no opt-out (Robert, 13 Nov 2026); every other entrant sees only their own result, and scores reach only the teacher. A class space is private to its members and names are pen names. Please confirm that joining is a sound basis for showing a child's pen name as Winner to their class, and whether the parent's consent text should mention competitions.
- **A pupil's scores in their own data export.** A teacher's scores, results and teacher points are stored under the teacher who wrote them, so today they are in the teacher's export and not in the pupil's. The pupil sees their result and their teacher points on screen. Please say whether the pupil's export (GDPR art. 15 and 20) must also carry the scores and results given to them.
- **Competition results in the class log.** The confirmed results, with every entrant's score per criterion, are written to the class's log, which only the teacher and our moderators read. A pupil who later leaves the class has their entry, scores and result deleted, but the log line stays. Please confirm how long that line may be kept.

### Found while building INACTIVE-2 and ARCHIVE-1: unused accounts and the story archive (11 and 12 Nov 2026)

- **The story archive.** Stories (with their Story Atlas) of an account closed after 5 unused years are kept in a private archive for 5 more years, with a scrambled email, the year of birth, the pen name and the username, so the owner can be recognised. Robert's position (12 Nov 2026): nobody in the archive is under 18 (the youngest joiner is 13, and the archive starts after 5 unused years), the details are kept only to give the stories back, and the terms say so when the account is made. Please confirm that is enough, given that the stories were written by a child (GDPR art. 17(1)(f), recital 65).
- **Claiming stories back.** A new account with the same confirmed email gives its year of birth and a pen name, username or story title; three wrong tries lock it for a day. Please confirm this identity check is enough.
- **Reminder emails to unused accounts.** Yearly, then every 3 months from the 4th year, then the closing notice; under-18s get them too. Please confirm they count as plain account notices, not marketing.

### Found while building WAITLIST-1: the waiting list (7 Nov 2026)

- **A new purpose in the privacy notice.** While the waiting list is on, an account is created and kept (email, username, year of birth, country, the "write, read or both" answer) before the person can use the app, so staff can let people in in waves, writers first and by country. The privacy notice needs a paragraph saying so, what the answer is used for and that the place is deleted with the account. No new legal version was published from the code; publishing one would ask everyone to accept again, so that step is left to Robert.
- **News emails.** A separate, unticked "Send me occasional news" box at sign-up and in Settings › Notifications; kept only for someone 18 or over (Q50; Law 506/2004 art. 12 on unsolicited commercial messages). Please confirm the wording counts as specific, informed consent and that the unsubscribe link in each news email is enough.
- **Unconfirmed accounts.** Every account whose email isn't confirmed is deleted after 7 days (`unconfirmed_account_days`), waiting accounts included (Robert, 7 Nov 2026). The same 7 days will apply to people who asked to hear when their market opens and are then emailed to activate their account. Please confirm this suits the record of processing.
- **"Founding member" badge.** Shown publicly on the profile of everyone who joined while the list was on. It reveals only that the account is an early one.

### Found while building LOOK-4c: suggesting writers and the parent's separate yes (6 Nov 2026)

- **Suggesting a writer to strangers (S1-E5-11).** New and rising writers and Writers to follow show a writer's username or pen name, avatar and stories to people who don't follow them. A writer is shown only while their own "Suggest me and my stories to other people" switch is on: on by default for adults, off by default for under-18s (DoD privacy). Featured this week shows only writers aged 16 or over. Please confirm consent is the right basis for adults and that the default-on switch for adults is acceptable.
- **The parent's separate yes for 13 to 15-year-olds (S1-E2-03b; Robert, 6 Nov 2026, D4).** A 13 to 15-year-old is suggested only when they turned the switch on and their parent or guardian also agreed, separately from the account's consent (GDPR art. 7 and 8): an optional, unticked box on the consent page, or a one-question page the child can ask us to email. The parent can take it back from the same page, or from the consent email's own link, and staff can withdraw it when asked (logged). Withdrawing the account's consent also ends it. Please confirm this separation is enough, and the wording on both pages and in the emails.
- **Notice versions.** The privacy notice (en and ro) gains one purpose paragraph for this. No new legal version was published from the code; publishing one would ask everyone to accept again, so that step is left to Robert.

### Found while building SAFE-4: pictures, appeals, moderator mail and the accessibility statement (30 Oct 2026)

- **A new processor for uploaded pictures.** Every picture goes to Arachnid Shield (hash matching against known child sexual abuse material) when it is uploaded; Sightengine was dropped on 2 Nov 2026. Please check the data processing agreement, the transfer to Canada, and the privacy notice lines that name it. Uploads stay off until this is done.
- **Closed for good (bundle ADMIN-2, part f1).** A banned account is closed, never deleted: its content is hidden and kept under the retention schedule as evidence, the email is blocked (normalised: no +tag, no Gmail dots) and throwaway email domains can't sign up. Please check the Pictures and Closed for good paragraphs in the terms and guidelines drafts, and how long a banned account's data may be kept (today: like any account's data, without the 14-day deletion).
- **A known abuse match.** The picture is never shown to staff, a same-day safeguarding case opens and the file is kept six months for the authorities. Please confirm the reporting duty (to whom, how fast) and the six-month preservation.
- **Appeals (DSA art. 20).** A person can appeal for six months; a different moderator decides where possible; overturning restores the item and lifts the sanction. Please confirm the six months and that "where possible" is enough for a very small team.
- **Moderator mail as the single point of contact for users (DSA art. 12).** Members write to one shared team inbox and replies are signed "The moderation team", never a name. Messages are kept one year after the thread's last update (**proposal**). Please confirm the retention and that unsigned replies are fine.
- **Rate limits.** New accounts (under 24 hours) get lower hourly limits and one link per comment. Please confirm this needs no line in the terms beyond the community guidelines' spam rule.
- **The accessibility statement.** A draft in both languages at `/legal/accessibility`. Please check whether the European Accessibility Act or Romanian law requires particular wording or a complaint route for a service of our size.

### Found while building SAFE-3: statements of reasons, removal orders and safeguarding (26 Oct 2026)

Needs the lawyer's review before launch.

- **Statements of reasons (DSA art. 17).** Every decision other than "No action" sends the person a statement in their language: what we did, the facts in the moderator's own words, the rule (our terms, or the law for illegal content and removal orders), that no automated tools made the decision (the picture check flags but never decides) and that they can challenge it for six months. Until the appeals story (S1-E10-07, after March) the challenge goes through Contact us. The internal complaint system (art. 20) and sending statements to the Commission's database (art. 24(5)) are online-platform duties that art. 19 lifts for micro and small enterprises; please confirm we qualify and that Contact us is enough for the invited test.
- **Telling the reporters (DSA art. 16(5)).** Reporters are told only "removed", "action taken" or "no breach", never which sanction the other person got. People who used the public form get the same by email. Please confirm this balance with the reported person's privacy.
- **Removal orders: one hour and the first order.** Regulation (EU) 2021/784 art. 3(2) says that for a provider that has never had an order, the authority must give at least 12 hours' notice of procedures and deadlines before the first one. We act within the hour regardless. Please confirm which Romanian authority is competent and which national law names it (our notes say Law 50/2024; not checked), and whether the 12-hour notice changes anything in our runbook.
- **Keeping removed content six months (TCO art. 6).** An item taken down under an order is kept six months (longer only at the authority's written request) and the purge jobs skip it. Please confirm the start date (the take-down) and that the same period may apply to the related data (the account's handle and timestamps).
- **Telling the uploader (TCO art. 11).** The uploader is told by default; the moderator unticks it only when the order says not to (art. 11(3)). Please confirm the wording and that the later lift of that restriction needs a manual follow-up, which the runbook asks for.
- **The evidence pack (DSA art. 18).** For a safeguarding case, staff with the safeguarding permission can download a file with the content, the account's handle, pen name, email, country, age group and sanction history, the reports and every timestamp, to hand to the police or a helpline. Building it is logged. Please confirm what may go into it without a formal request from the authority, and the legal basis for handing over a minor's email.
- **The on-call rota.** It records who on the team is on call and when. Please confirm this needs only a line in the staff privacy notice.

### Found while building S1-E2-12 and S1-E2-13 (26 Oct 2026)

The line is in `content/legal/` but not yet in a published legal version: it goes live with the next version you approve.
- **The joining anniversary.** In the month someone joined, the signed-in greeting says "Happy anniversary". It uses only the account's creation date, which we already keep. Please confirm no new notice line is needed.

### Found while building S1-E10-08 (24 Oct 2026)

Please confirm this note is not defamatory towards the writer when the finding is wrong, that it needs a statement of reasons to the writer (DSA art. 17, built in S1-E10-04) and that the writer can appeal it (S1-E10-07).
