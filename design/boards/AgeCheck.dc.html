How it works

Three short steps

  1. 1Yoti’s page opens in a new tab.
  2. 2You take a selfie or scan your ID there.
  3. 3You come back to Ghemotoc and see your result.

Until the check is done, you can read but not post. If it isn’t done within 14 days of joining, we delete the account and your year (and month) of birth.
