The register

“Maybe she forgot to write him down,” Teo said. He was whispering, and Teo never whispers.

“She doesn’t forget,” Mara said. “She counts us the whole way down. Fifteen, every Thursday since October.”

Teo didn’t answer. He was looking at the fifteenth name, written in a hand none of them knew.

“She doesn’t forget,” Mara said. “She counts us the whole way down…”
