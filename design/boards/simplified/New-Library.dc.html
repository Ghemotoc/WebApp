Comes with the apps

Offline reading comes with the ghemotoc apps for computers and phones, after the March 2027 test. Downloads stay locked inside the app, so they can’t be opened as files anywhere else, and they’re removed after 30 days or straight away when you log out.

Nothing to download on the website. Until the apps arrive, you read online here, on any device you log in on.
