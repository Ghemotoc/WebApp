The Salt Mine Choir

by inkwolf

1.2k reads418 likes640 comments15 chapters31.4k wordsUpdated 16 Oct 2026

Every Thursday, 14 teenagers take the lift 200 metres down into an old salt mine to rehearse. Their choir director counts them on the way down.

One night she counts 15, and nobody can say where the new boy came from, least of all the register.
