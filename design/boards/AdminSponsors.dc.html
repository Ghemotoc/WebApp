Sponsors and banners

Set up sponsor campaigns, check every creative before it goes live and send each sponsor a simple report. A banner matches the page, not the person reading it, so everyone on the same page sees the same banner.

Page context only A banner is picked by the page’s category, tag, language and market. Nothing about the reader is used.
A person approves every creative Nothing goes live until a member of staff checks it is suitable for under-18s.
Never inside the text At most one banner below a chapter. No pop-ups, nothing full screen.
Sponsors don’t change rankings Sponsors have no say over what we publish, rank or remove. Every sponsor contract says so.

Placements

Placements

Book a sponsor into a slot here. Ad-network ads need no booking: they fill whatever is left, automatically. Every public page has at most 2 ad slots, and class spaces have none.

How each slot is filled, every time a page loads
  1. 1A direct sponsor booked for that slot and those dates, when the page matches the booking’s category, tag and language. Labelled “Sponsored” with the sponsor’s name.
  2. 2A house ad for our own contests or landing pages, when one is scheduled for that slot.
  3. 3The ad network, automatically, only if it’s switched on below and the vendor never personalises. Labelled “Ad”.
  4. 4Otherwise a house ad, so the slot is never empty and the page never jumps.
Class spaces have no slots at all: no ads, no sponsored items and no sponsor credits.

Ad-network ads · after March

“Ad” and “Sponsored” are two different things

Unsold banner slots can be filled from an ad network, but only one that never personalises ads, for every reader of every age. That keeps “No tracking ads” true.

OPEN: which ad network
AD Ad-network ads From [Ad network], non-personalised only. Labelled “Ad” (“Reclamă”). They fill a slot no sponsor has and load only when the slot comes into view.
SPONSORED Direct sponsors Our own sponsors, such as a bookshop or a ministry, set up on this page. Labelled “Sponsored” (“Sponsorizat”) with the sponsor’s name.
Rules for ad-network code
  • Only in the banner slot. Never in chapter text, full screen or a class space.
  • No cookie and nothing read from the device unless the reader agreed. If the network can’t promise that, the slot shows our own banner.
  • Added only through a written tech decision and the list of processors.
Blocked categories
  • Alcohol
  • Gambling
  • Tobacco and vaping
  • Dating
  • Weight loss
  • Anything unsuitable for under-18s
Everyone sees the same kind of ads, so every block applies to every reader.
Fill empty slots with ad-network ads Off until the network is chosen and a lawyer has checked it

The ad network fills only slots that aren’t booked or covered by a house ad. We never send it anything about the reader. See Placements.
