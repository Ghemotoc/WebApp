Landing pages

Make a page for a contest, a campaign or something people need to know. Pages are built only from fixed blocks, so every page stays safe, readable and in our colours. Story S1-E11-14.

Home page Notice banners Seasonal doodles Help and questions Landing pages
Fixed blocks only No free HTML, no custom code and no custom colours. Pick blocks, fill them in and choose one of four themes.
Both languages English and Română are edited side by side. A page can’t go live with either one missing.
Every picture is checked Each picture needs alt text in both languages and must pass the picture check before anyone sees it.
Safe for the youngest entrant Featured stories must suit the youngest age group allowed to enter. We never show who entered by age.

Ads on landing pages

One slot, our own banners

OPEN: ad-network ads

Proposal: no ad-network ads on landing pages. A landing page has one wide slot below the first content. It shows the contest sponsor’s “Sponsored” banner or one of our own house ads, nothing else.

  • Contest pages are made for writers who can be under 18, and the sponsor already pays for the page.
  • The usual rules still apply: never full screen, never a pop-up, never two ads in view.
  • Needs Robert’s decision before S1-E11-14 is ready.
