DOODLE SET · FINE INK AND COLOUR

100 hand-drawn doodles

These doodles decorate the website, empty states, certificates and social posts. Seasonal ones rotate through the year. Their colours follow the palette review. The source files are on Robert’s Desktop in “Doodles v4”, in four styles.

How to use them

  1. Decorative only and hidden from screen readers.
  2. Never behind or over text.
  3. A few per screen, in mixed sizes. Doodle icons replace ordinary icons, except arrows and controls.
  4. None on the chapter reader, sharing, reporting or staff screens.
  5. Seasonal ones switch by date and occasion, like the Discover greeting and the Search tags.
