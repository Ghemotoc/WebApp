The Salt Mine Choir · chapter 14

The register

by inkwolf · 9 min read · Fiction

Mara ran her finger down the page for each Thursday. Corina, Teo, Bianca, the two Stan brothers, herself. Fourteen names every week, all in Mrs Dobre’s square handwriting, all ticked. She checked September again, then October, then the week of the power cut when only nine of them came.

There was no Ilie. There was no fifteenth name at all.

“Maybe she forgot to write him down,” Teo said. He was whispering, and Teo never whispers.

Above them the lift groaned on its cable. Somebody was coming down, and the register said nobody else was expected.
