Legal basis

What the GDPR asks of us

Art 12
Answer without undue delay and within one month of receiving the request. For complex or many requests we can take two more months, but we must tell the person within the first month and say why. If we have reasonable doubts about who is asking, we can ask for more information first.
Art 15
Access: a copy of their personal data. The copy must not harm other people’s rights, so we leave out others’ personal data where needed.
Art 17
Deletion: we delete their data unless we must keep it, for example as evidence under a legal order.
Art 20
Portability: the data they gave us, in a common machine-readable format (JSON).
Art 16
Correction: we fix personal data that is wrong.

The first copy is free. Decided: a 13 to 15-year-old can download their own data from their own signed-in account. A request made for a child by anyone else goes through the parent or guardian on the consent record. A lawyer checks the minors’ points before March.
