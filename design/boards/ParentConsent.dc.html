What Ghemotoc is

A free place to write and read stories, poems and essays. Writers keep the rights to their work.

What we keep

Their email, their username, a pen name if they choose one, their year of birth (and birth month, if we needed it) and the age check result. We never ask for their real name.

How we protect under-18s
  • Adults can never message anyone under 16. If they’re 16 or 17, messages from adults are off unless they turn them on.
  • They can block and report anyone. A person reads every report.
  • Stories are rated All ages, 13+, 16+ or 18+. Your child only sees stories rated for their age.
  • Ads match the page and are the same for everyone. They never use your child’s profile.

Read the Privacy notice (version 1.0) and the Terms (version 1.0). Both are in Romanian and English.
