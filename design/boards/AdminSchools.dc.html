Class spaces

Class spaces

A class space is private and works by invitation: it doesn’t appear in search, Discover or public lists, and only members can open its posts and stories. It follows the people in the room, so any age mix is fine (15, 16, 17 and older classmates) and it is never split at 16. The teacher is a member. It locks at the end of the academic year: no new posts, and members can still read it for as long as they have an account. No ads, sponsored items or sponsor credits, ever.
