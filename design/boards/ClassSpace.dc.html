How a class space starts

  1. 1The teacher gets a Teacher badgeThey ask for it in Settings with a school email or a letter from the school. Our staff check it.
  2. 2They create a space and pick “Class space”Same form as any space. The class space is private and comes with an invite code.
  3. 3Students join with the codeThe teacher writes the code on the board or shares the link. Students type it in Forums › Join a class.

For the March test, our staff set up class spaces for the pilot schools by hand. Teachers do it themselves after March.
