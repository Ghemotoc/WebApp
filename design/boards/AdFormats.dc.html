Ads and sponsors

How ads and sponsors show up

Ghemotoc is free to use. Ads and sponsors help pay for it. These are all the places they can appear, and the rules every one of them follows. Two labels tell them apart: “Ad” marks ads from ad networks, and “Sponsored” marks items from our own sponsors. Class spaces never show ads, sponsored items or sponsor credits.

For sponsors
Never inside the text of a story
Never full screen and never a pop-up
Never personalised, no tracking, no profiles
The same ads for everyone, including under-18s
Every format

Where you might see them

Banner after a chapter

Below the last line. One per chapter at most.

Ads from ad networks are labelled “Ad” (“Reclamă” in Romanian) and show who placed them. They are never personalised, for anyone.

Card in a list of stories

One card in a row of stories on Discover

Labelled “Sponsored”, because it comes from one of our sponsors. It looks different from a story on purpose, so nobody mistakes it for one.

Strip between shelves

One line between two rows of stories

Labelled “Sponsored”, with the sponsor’s name, like every item from our sponsors.

Category “presented by” a sponsor

One line at the top of a category

The sponsor’s name sits in the header. Which stories show, and in what order, stays exactly the same.

Featured slide

One of the slides at the top of Discover

Used for a sponsored contest or event, never to push a paid story up the list.

Sponsored writing contest

On Home and in Forums

The sponsor’s name is shown on the contest. The rules are the same for every entry.

Wide banner

Between two shelves or groups

The full width of the page, up to 970 px wide and 120 px tall (100 px on phones). After the first row of stories or the first forum group, never above it.

Portrait

Wide screens only, beside search results

A tall ad in the side column, 300 × 600. It shows only on screens 1200 px and wider, and never on phones or tablets.

Below the reply box

In public forum threads

One wide ad below the reply box at the end of a thread, plus one side ad on wide screens. Never between replies. No picture of a person and no reply button, so it never looks like a post.

Not too many

How many ads

Enough to help pay for Ghemotoc, never so many that they get in the way of reading. Ads from ad networks are never personalised, for anyone, and class spaces never show ads.

  • Two at most on a page: one wide banner and one smaller ad, such as a card in a list, a portrait beside search results or a side ad in a forum thread.
  • One on screen at a time. Never two side by side or one straight after another.
  • Never first. Always below the page heading and the first row of stories or posts.
  • Lists and threads: at most one ad card in every 12 stories, never in first place. In a thread, one wide ad below the reply box and one side ad on wide screens, never between replies.
  • Only on public pages: Discover, Search, Classics, See all, story pages, shared links, app-only pages, your library, forums, threads, public spaces and profiles. Never in class spaces, the writing tools, messages, settings or help pages.

How it works

How we choose and check every ad

What decides the ad you see

Only the page you are on: its category, its tags and its language. Never your age, your reading history or your device. Ads are never personalised, for anyone.

What we check first

A person on our team checks every sponsored item before it goes live, including a text description for every picture. For ad networks, we turn off every category that isn’t fine for under-18s, such as alcohol, gambling, tobacco and dating.

What sponsors can’t do

Sponsors have no say over what we publish, how we rank stories or what we remove. That is written into every sponsor contract.

For sponsors

Sponsor a category, a contest or a featured slide

Send us your name, one image with a text description, a link, where you want to appear and the dates. You get a daily count of views and clicks, counted without cookies or user IDs.
