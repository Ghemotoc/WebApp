For a translation, the translator counts too: the later of the two deaths decides. For example, Hamlet is free everywhere, but a Romanian translation whose translator died in 1980 isn’t free in Romania, the EU or the UK until 1 January 2051. Modern notes and introductions can still be protected.

Law 8/1996 art. 28(1) and 34; research G5. A lawyer checks this test before launch (lawyer’s list, section 6).
