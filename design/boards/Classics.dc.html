Romanian and world classics in the public domain. Read, follow and comment on them like any other story.

How we check

Our staff check every classic before it goes up. The writer died more than 70 years ago, and so did any translator, so the text is in the public domain in Romania and the EU. We use editions without modern notes or introductions, because those can still be protected.

Translations and modern editions can still be under copyright, so world classics stay in their original language.
