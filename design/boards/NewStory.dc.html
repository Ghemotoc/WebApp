Square cover This step opens by itself once your tall cover is added. The square starts in the middle. Drag it to choose which part to keep, and zoom in if you like. Words outside the square are cut, so check it before you save.
Design it in Canva(opens a new tab)

Canva opens a blank cover at the right size. When you press Return in Canva, your cover comes back here. You need your own Canva account, and Canva’s rules apply there. (Idea for later, not in the March test.)

Design note: the dashed square is a logo slot. The built app uses Canva’s official, full-colour logo from its brand kit, unchanged.

  • Sizes: tall 1600 × 2560 pixels (smallest 800 × 1280), square 1600 × 1600 (smallest 800 × 800).
  • No square cover? We use the middle of your tall one until you add one.
  • We show your picture exactly as it is, with no title or name added on top, so put them on the picture yourself if you want them on the cover. Your title and pen name still appear under the cover everywhere.
  • Keep any words on your picture away from the edges. Small screens crop a little.
  • JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB.
  • We remove location data from the file.
  • Every picture is checked before it shows. Until then, only you can see it.
