If you’re stuck

Recovery and first sign-in

New phone or lost phone. Use the backup codes Google gave you. If you have none, an owner resets your two-step check after checking it’s really you on a video call. The reset is saved in the audit log with who did it and why.
First sign-in. An owner creates your Google Workspace account and you turn on Google’s two-step check together. Your access starts only after you’ve signed the confidentiality terms in your contract.
Your staff identity is separate. You can also have a personal Ghemotoc account to write and read, but it’s a different identity from your staff account: you log in to it with your member log-in, it never holds a staff role and staff powers never reach it.
Who gets a staff account. Nobody can sign up as staff. An owner or admin gives the role, and every role change is logged.
Staff roles: moderator, support, content editor, admin and owner. A space moderator looks after one space and is a member role, not a staff role.
