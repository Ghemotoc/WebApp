Space rules

  1. Fantasy only. Science fiction goes in the main forums.
  2. When you ask for feedback, say what kind you want.
  3. Give feedback on two chapters before you post your third.
  4. Mark spoilers for published books.

The Ghemotoc community guidelines apply here too.

Create a space

Anyone can create a space, and you become its owner. Because you’re 16 or over, your space is for members 16 and over, and anyone, with or without an account, can read it. You can change everything below later.

Who can join

  • Spaces keep age groups apart. A space started by someone under 16 takes only under-16s. A space started by someone 16 or over takes only people 16 and over.
  • Under-16s choose who can read their space: only members, or everybody. Adults can read it only if the owner made it public to everybody.
  • When a member turns 16, they stay in the spaces they joined before, but they can’t start or join under-16 spaces.
  • Class spaces are the one exception: the teacher invites the class, whatever their ages.
