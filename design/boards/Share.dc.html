Chapter 14 of 15
The register
by inkwolf · 9 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

Mrs Dobre kept the register in a green folder in the cupboard by the lift, next to the spare tuning fork and a tin of throat sweets. At 18:10 she went up to the ticket office to make a phone call, and Mara opened the cupboard.

“Two minutes,” Teo said. He stood in the doorway, where he could see the lift. In the big hall behind them, the others were singing the first eight bars of ‘Foaie verde’ for the fourth time.

Mara ran her finger down the page for each Thursday. Corina, Teo, Bianca, the two Stan brothers, herself. Fourteen names every week, all in Mrs Dobre’s square handwriting, all ticked. She checked September again, then October, then the week of the power cut when only nine of them came.

There was no Ilie. There was no fifteenth name at all.

“Maybe she forgot to write him down,” Teo said. He was whispering, and Teo never whispers.

“She doesn’t forget,” Mara said. “She counts us the whole way down. Fifteen, every Thursday since October. I’ve watched her do it.”

Mara took a photo of the September page and the October page with her phone. Her hands were shaking, so she took each one twice.

Above them the lift groaned on its cable. Somebody was coming down, and the register said nobody else was expected.
