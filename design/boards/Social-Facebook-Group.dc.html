If parents ask what Ghemotoc is like for 13 to 17-year-olds, here’s a summary you can forward to them.

Messages from adults

Adults can never message under-16s. For 16 and 17-year-olds it’s off by default, and only the teen can turn it on.

Ads

No tracking ads. Banners match the chapter, not the reader, so everyone sees the same ones. Never inside the story text and never full screen.

Age check

Sign-up asks for year of birth, then Yoti does the age check. We only get the result, never a photo. The minimum age is 13, and under-16s also need consent from a parent or guardian, by email.

Pen name

Optional. Writers can use a pen name instead of their real name.

Report button

On every story, comment and message. A person reads every report and explains the decision.
