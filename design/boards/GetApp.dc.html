The apps come after the web launch

Ghemotoc starts on the web, so you can read and write in your browser on any phone, tablet or computer. The desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux and the iPhone and Android apps follow.

In your browser, from day one

Use Ghemotoc in your browser

Go to ghemotoc.com on any phone, tablet or computer and log in. Until the apps are out, you can read whole chapters and write everything in the browser. For a quick way back, put Ghemotoc on your home screen or bookmark it.
