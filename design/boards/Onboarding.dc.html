Step 3 of 3 · for under-18s

Staying safe here

Block someone

Open their profile or your chat, then press Block. They can’t message, mention or follow you, and they aren’t told.

Report anything

Every story, comment, post and message has a Report button. A person reads every report.

Who can message you

Adults can never message anyone under 16. If you’re 16 or 17, messages from adults are off unless you turn them on. Change this any time in Settings.

The community guidelines, in short
  • Be kind. No bullying, threats or hate.
  • Post your own work. You’re responsible for what you publish.
  • Keep your address, school and phone number private.
  • Rate your stories: All ages, 13+, 16+ or 18+.
