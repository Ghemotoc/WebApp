Parental consent for under-16s On from launch

Accounts aged 13 to 15 stay inactive until a parent or guardian agrees by email. They can’t post, comment or message before that. Consent is our legal basis, so this stays on. Use the switch only in an emergency, and a lawyer reviews it before March.

Adults can never message anyone under 16. If you’re 16 or 17, messages from adults are off unless you turn them on. Only the teen can switch it, in their own Settings. Staff, parents and adults can’t, and no setting here changes it.

Markets and languages

Markets and languages

Romania opens first, with the interface in Romanian and English in the language menu. Other countries open later, one by one, each after its legal check. Australia stays closed to under-16s until a lawyer says otherwise. Statuses below are a pre-launch example. Open: people can use it. Ready to open: every check is done and it waits for an admin. Closed: not available yet.

A language opens only when moderators who read it are on the rota. Writers pick a story’s language from the open ones. Closed languages show as “Coming when we have moderators for it”. The interface stays in English and Română.

Support tickets

Support

Every email to the support address, every Contact us message and every in-app message becomes a ticket in Support. A ready-made helpdesk runs behind our screens through its API, so staff never leave the admin site.

Open Support

Replies go out from this address with the ticket number in the subject. A user’s email reply threads back into the same ticket.

Romanian time. Shown on the Contact us form and used for the first-reply timer. Bank holidays in Romania don’t count.

The timer in Support turns gold at 75% of the target and red when it’s missed.

A ticket waiting on the user is solved after this many days, with a polite email. If they reply later, it reopens.
