  • Mark as spam moves it to Spam. No reply is sent, and the sender’s later emails go there too.

How Support works

  • Replies go out from support@ghemotoc.com with the ticket number in the subject. Email replies thread back into the same ticket.
  • A ticket waiting on the user is solved after 7 days, with a polite email. A reply reopens it; a reply to a closed ticket starts a new one linked to it.
  • Tickets are kept for 2 years after they close and are included in the person’s data export.
  • Tickets run on a ready-made helpdesk connected through its API (product to be chosen). Staff work only in these screens, and every view and action is in the audit log.
  • People see their tickets under “Your support requests” in Settings.
  • Tickets never replace reports. Unlawful content and authority removal orders go through the report form.
