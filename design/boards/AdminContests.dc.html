Contests

Set up a writing contest, check the entries, pick the winners and publish the results. Each contest has its own landing page under Website. Writers enter from their own story settings, so staff never touch an entry.

New contest Landing pages
People judge, not votesProposal Our staff and named partner judges pick the winners. There is no public vote.
Sponsors don’t judge A sponsor can pay for a prize and get a credit. It has no say in who wins. Every sponsor contract says so.
Pen names only Staff see an age group, never a year of birth. Results show pen names, never age, and a school only when the writer agreed.
Rules first A contest can’t open until its rules are written and a lawyer has reviewed them.
