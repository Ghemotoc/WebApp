My grandmother has written down the weather every morning since 1971. Temperature, wind, cloud, and one word for how she slept. The notebooks fill a whole shelf in the hall, and nobody is allowed to dust them.

The morning she went into hospital, I opened that day’s page and found she had already filled it in. Tomorrow’s too. And the day after that, in pencil, with a little drawing of a storm.

Mum said it was nothing. “She’s 81. She gets her days mixed up.” Then she went back to packing Bunica’s nightie and reading glasses into a plastic bag.

But Bunica never gets her days mixed up. She knows the price of bread in three different shops. She knows my timetable better than I do.

* * *

On Tuesday it was 14 degrees, light wind from the north, no clouds. Exactly what she had written. Under it, in the space for how she slept, it said “badly”.

I took a photo of the page and sent it to my cousin Radu. He wrote back straight away: “ok that’s creepy. what does thursday say”

Thursday was the one in pencil. Thursday was the storm.
