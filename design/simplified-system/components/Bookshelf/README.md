# Bookshelf

A bookshelf is a collection a member makes: up to 10 books, face-out, in the same row as every other row of covers (Shelf). Members call them bookshelves (Robert, 20 Nov 2026; "reading lists" in the code today). There is no plank and no wood (Robert, 20 Nov 2026).

**What the consumer provides:** the bookshelf's name, its books (cover, title, pen name, link), the member's age band and plan, and whether the viewer owns it.

**Capacity you can see.** Free members get 10 places per bookshelf, so the places a book has not taken yet show as ghosts (`line-dashed`) after the books. The first ghost holds a + that leads to stories. The meta line says it in words: "7 of 10 books · Public"; a full bookshelf says "10 of 10 books · Full" in the same quiet style, never a red warning.

**Who can see a bookshelf** (Robert, 20 Nov 2026)

| Member | Bookshelves | Meta line | The line under the bookshelves |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under 18 | Private, with no switch | Private | "Only you can see a bookshelf." |
| 18 and over, free plan | Public: everyone can see them | Public | "Everyone can see a bookshelf. Premium keeps it private." |
| 18 and over, premium | Private or Public, chosen per bookshelf | Private or Public | None |

- **Turning 18:** a notice 30 days before the birthday and another on the day, at the top of the Library and in Notifications. Then the bookshelves turn public, unless the member goes premium or moves the books. Before: "From 3 March, everyone can see a bookshelf. Premium keeps them private. Saved for later stays private either way." On the day: "Everyone can now see a bookshelf. Premium keeps them private."
- **Always private, for everyone:** Liked and the automatic Library rows (Reading, Saved for later, Finished). They are rows above the bookshelves, not bookshelves, so they never count toward the three.
- **In the Library** bookshelves sit one under another, `space-12` apart, then a quiet **New bookshelf** button while there is room, and the limit said once: "2 of 3 bookshelves · Free members keep 3 bookshelves of 10 books". With three, the button goes and the line says "3 of 3 bookshelves".
- The name is in `subheading` and links to the bookshelf's own page, where each book also gets a Remove button.
- Each book is a link named "Title by pen name"; the empty places are hidden from screen readers, except the first, which is the link to find stories.

**Lawyer:** public bookshelves on the free plan, paying for privacy and the switch at 18 are on the lawyer list (20 Nov 2026).

**Don't:** draw a plank, a cabinet or wood; show more than 10 places on a free bookshelf; show the limit as an error; hide existing bookshelves when a plan changes; or make a bookshelf public before 18.
