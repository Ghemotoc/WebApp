# AddToBookshelf

The glass sheet that opens from **Add to a bookshelf** on a story page: each bookshelf with how full it is, and a way to start a new one.

- One row per bookshelf: name (`label`), fullness (`meta`, "7 of 10"), and a button: + to add, a check when the book is already there (pressing it again takes the book off).
- A full bookshelf stays in the list, saying "10 of 10 · Full" beside a lock, so the member sees why and can make room. Nothing is dimmed: the words say it.
- **New bookshelf** is the last row; at the free limit it says "3 of 3 bookshelves".
- The limit is written once at the bottom in the meta style, and under it who can see a bookshelf: "Everyone can see a bookshelf. Premium keeps it private." for members of 18 and over on the free plan, "Only you can see a bookshelf." under 18, and nothing for premium members, who choose per bookshelf.

**Don't:** hide full bookshelves, put a paywall inside the sheet, or close the sheet before the member sees the check.
