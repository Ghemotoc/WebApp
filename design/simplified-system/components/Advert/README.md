# Advert

Adverts are AdSense limited ads, the same for everyone: nothing is personalised. The reader carries two (Robert, 20 Nov 2026).

| Slot | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Box (`gh-ad--box`) | 300 × 250 | The reader's side column, under About this story |
| Banner (`gh-ad--banner`) | 728 × 90 | Under the chapter navigation, before the chapter's comments |

- The slot says "Advertisement" above the ad, so an ad never passes for a story, a card or a button.
- **No adverts** on stories marked for sexual content, nudity or graphic violence, or in class spaces, and no ads for gambling, alcohol, politics or nicotine.
- In previews the slot is a dashed outline (`line-dashed`) with its size written in it.

**Don't:** put an advert inside the text, inside a row of covers or in the floating layer, or let it move the text once the page has loaded (keep its space from the start).
