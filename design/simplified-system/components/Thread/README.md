# Thread

One 2px line in the `thread` colour (violet by day, white at night) that does the jobs boxes, eyebrows and badges do today.

**Uses**
- Progress under covers on Continue reading, and the writing month meter in Home's hero. In the reader the minutes left in the chapter bar are the progress, so there is no line there (Robert, 20 Nov 2026).
- The current tab on the phone tab bar, and the unread row in Notifications.
- The current chapter in the chapter dots (ReaderPage).
- The line of a line chart (LineChart).
- A faint 9% tint around the paragraph whose comments are open (`gh-para-open`).

It suits the name, a little ball of thread, and the logo, a cat curled into one.

**Don't:** thicken it, animate it for show, or use it as a border around boxes. Progress lines also carry their value as text or ARIA, never colour alone.
