## What changed
- **Database (`20261025100001`):**
  - The setting `teaser_length` = 600 (decided 23 Oct 2026).
  - `teaser_of(body, limit)`: the first whole paragraphs up to 600 words. When the first paragraph alone is longer, its first 600 words end with "…".
  - `get_chapter()` now sends a signed-out visitor only the teaser, with `is_teaser` and `text_continues`. The cut happens in the database, so the rest of the chapter never reaches their browser. Signed-in readers still get the whole chapter (Q35).
  - `get_link_preview(story, chapter)` gives the title and rating always. For All ages and 13+ it adds the blurb, pen name, cover and chapter title. For 16+ and 18+ it gives nothing more: visitors count as under 16 (decided 23 Oct 2026, Q6: "only the title of 16+ and 18+ stories"). It never returns the user ID or chapter text.
- **Teaser page (board 3.8):** a signed-out visitor at `/s/<story>/<chapter>` sees:
  - the story card: the cover, "Shared with you · chapter 1 of 2", the story's title as the H1, the pen name, the blurb and the pills;
  - the chapter's heading and "n min read · You're reading the first part";
  - the teaser, fading out when the chapter goes on;
  - "Log in to read on", with the March wording for invited adults;
  - the copy notice. The teaser is chapter text, so right-click and copying are off on it too (S1-E4-06a).
  - Doodles: the open book on the card and the heart balloon on the prompt, from 1000 px up, clear of all text.
- **16+ and 18+:** the title and "No preview for this story". The notice names the real rating and offers "Log in to check if you can read it". It shows on the chapter address and on the story page.
- **Link previews and search engines (`functions/s/[[path]].ts` → `server/link-preview.ts`):**
  - Every page under `/s/` gets:
    - `<title>`;
    - `og:title` ("Story · Chapter");
    - `og:description` ("de <pen name> · <blurb>", in the story's language);
    - `og:image` (`/link-previews/<cover colour>.png`);
    - `og:url` (no query, so no tracking codes);
    - `og:site_name` (read from the built page's title, never written in code);
    - a Twitter summary card.
  - Story pages carry JSON-LD with the title, blurb and pen name only.
  - Chapters carry `noindex` in the page and in an `X-Robots-Tag` header (Q36).
  - Every value is HTML-escaped. If the database can't be reached, the page still loads, just without tags.
  - Locally, `server/pages-functions-plugin.ts` runs the same code for `npm run dev`, `npm run preview` and the e2e tests.
- **The pictures:**
  - `scripts/make-link-preview-images.ts` writes nine 400 × 400 squares, about 1 KB each, using only Node.
  - Their colours live in `server/link-preview-palette.ts`. A test fails if they ever drift from the cover palette.
  - A drawn cover gets its own colour. Automatic covers and 16+ and 18+ stories get plum.
- No new dependency. satori and resvg are not added.

## Hand-test steps (as a non-developer)
1. As `adult_writer`, publish a chapter longer than about two pages, and a second chapter.
2. Open the first chapter's address in a private window (not logged in). Check:
   - the story card with the cover, title, your pen name and blurb;
   - "Shared with you · chapter 1 of 2";
   - the first part of the chapter fading out;
   - "1 more chapter" and "Log in to read on".
3. Right-click the text: no menu. The copy notice sits at the bottom.
4. Log in as `teacher` and open the same address: the whole chapter, in the normal reader.
5. Paste the chapter's link into WhatsApp (or Telegram, or a Slack message). The card shows "Stația meteo · <chapter>", "de <pen name> · <blurb>" and a small square in the cover's colour.
6. In story settings, rate the story 18+. In the private window, reload the chapter and the story page: just the title and "No preview for this story… rated it 18+". Paste the link again: the title and "Clasificată 18+…", with no blurb. Set the rating back.
7. Read the Romanian copy:
   - "Trimis ție · capitolul 1 din 2";
   - "Citești prima parte";
   - "Conectează-te ca să citești mai departe" and its paragraph;
   - "Nicio previzualizare pentru această poveste" and its line;
   - the description "Clasificată 18+. Conectează-te ca să vezi dacă o poți citi."
