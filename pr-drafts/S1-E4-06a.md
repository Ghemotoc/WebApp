## What changed
- **`ProtectedChapterText`** wraps the chapter text in the reader and cancels four browser events on that text only: `contextmenu` (right-click), `copy`, `cut` and `dragstart` (dragging text out).
  - The listeners are native (`addEventListener`), so the wrapper stays a plain, non-interactive element.
  - The title, the chapter bar and the rest of the page copy as usual.
- **`user-select: none` is not used** (the plan, approved 23 Oct 2026). Readers can still select text to keep their place or to use the browser's find, screen readers still read and move through the text, and zoom and text size are untouched. This is the S1-E4-06 criterion and WCAG 2.2.
- **`CopyNotice`** sits under the chapter, before the banner slot, with the board's wording: "On the web, right-click and copying are turned off for chapters. That makes copying harder, but it can't stop screenshots. <pen name> keeps all the rights to this story." The Romanian version is in `reading.json`.
- **On Publish**, under the existing note, the writer reads: "Signed-in readers can read this chapter in the browser until the apps exist. Copying is made harder; screenshots still work on the web." The Romanian version is in `editor.json`.
- **`copyWording.test.ts`** reads every locale file in both languages and fails on any promise the web can't keep. It bans "can't be copied", "cannot be copied", "copy-proof" and "screenshots are blocked", and their Romanian equivalents ("nu poate fi copiat", "capturile de ecran sunt blocate", …). Q9: "makes copying harder", never "can't be copied".
- No migration.
