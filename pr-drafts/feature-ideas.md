# Feature ideas for Robert (running list)

Ideas I notice while building. Each morning at 9:00 CET the new ones go to Robert as one
document; he picks what joins the backlog. Nothing here is built unless Robert says so.

Format when sent: a user story (As a, I want, so that), Given/When/Then criteria, size (S about 3 h, M about 12 h), bundle and why it is good.

## Not yet sent

- **Reading streaks without pressure.** A private "you read 5 days this week" note in the Library, never a
  public badge and never a guilt notification. Helps readers come back; S; fits READ-4 or later.
- **"Finished" shelf thank-you.** When a reader moves a story to Finished, offer a one-tap "Tell the writer you
  finished it" that sends a kind, fixed message (no free text, so no moderation load). Writers learn who
  finished; S; fits READ-4.
- **Place shown on the story page's chapter list.** A small "you are here, 42%" mark beside the chapter the
  reader stopped in. Uses data the library already keeps; S; fits READ-4.
- **Writer dashboard: "readers finishing each chapter".** Aggregate only (never names), from the saved places:
  where readers stop most. Helps writers find slow chapters; M; fits WRITE-2.
- **Library export.** Include the reader's shelves in "Download my data" (it is personal data anyway). S; fits
  PRIVACY.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible as a fourth reading font.** Self-hosted (OFL licence, no third-party script), built for
  low-vision readers. The board already names it for the apps; it can work in the browser now. S; fits READ-4 or later.
- **Night theme by the clock.** An option to switch the chapter to Night after a chosen hour (for example 21:00),
  using only the device's clock. Kinder for bedtime readers, many of them teens; S.
- **Reading ruler.** A soft band that highlights the current line, toggled in Reading settings. Helps readers with
  dyslexia or ADHD keep their place; M.
- **Settings preview sentence.** A short sample paragraph at the top of the panel, so readers on a long chapter
  see the change without closing the panel; S.
