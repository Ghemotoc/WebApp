Three of the 31 Oct leftovers below went out in this one; "Where readers stop" was already IDEA-20261031-5.

### Furthest place for an exact read-through (idea, 31 Oct 2026)
As a writer, I want the read-through rate to count every reader who reached the end, even if they scrolled back up afterwards, so that the number matches what really happened. Why: the dashboard (S1-E3-07) uses each reader's latest place; keeping a "furthest place" beside it is one column and makes the rate exact.

### A "Where readers stop" line per chapter (idea, 31 Oct 2026)
As a writer, I want to see roughly where in a chapter readers stop (first, middle or last third, as totals with the "fewer than 5" rule), so that I know which scenes lose people. Why: the places are already saved for the Library; grouping them into thirds keeps it aggregate and gives writers something they can act on.

### Cover templates with the title already placed (idea, 31 Oct 2026)
As a writer, I want "Design it in Canva" to start from a cover template with my title and pen name already on it, so that I only have to pick a picture and colours. Why: S1-E3-12 opens a blank 1600 × 2560 design; Canva Connect can fill brand templates from data, and a ready-made layout would give writers who aren't designers a good cover in a few minutes.

### The settings tab notices the new cover by itself (idea, 31 Oct 2026)
As a writer, I want my story's settings to show the cover I just made in Canva as soon as I go back to that tab, so that I'm not left wondering whether it worked. Why: the cover comes back in the tab Canva used; a small "refresh on focus" for the settings form closes the loop without a page reload.

### Sent 31 Oct 2026 (/mnt/project-files/feature-ideas/2026-10-31.md)

- **Highlights writers can see.** Readers mark a line they loved; the writer sees a soft heat map of the
  most-loved paragraphs (counts only, never who). Gives writers feedback without asking readers to
  write a comment; M; after paragraph comments.
- **Reply to a paragraph from the notification.** The comment notification links straight to the paragraph
  with its thread open (`/s/<story>/<chapter>/p4?thread`), so the writer answers in context; S.
- **Reading streaks without pressure.** A private "you read 5 days this week" note in the Library, never a
  public badge and never a guilt notification. Helps readers come back; S; fits READ-4 or later.
- **"Finished" shelf thank-you.** When a reader moves a story to Finished, offer a one-tap "Tell the writer you
  finished it" that sends a kind, fixed message (no free text, so no moderation load). Writers learn who
  finished; S; fits READ-4.
- **Place shown on the story page's chapter list.** A small "you are here, 42%" mark beside the chapter the
  reader stopped in. Uses data the library already keeps; S; fits READ-4.
- **Writer dashboard: "readers finishing each chapter".** Aggregate only (never names), from the saved places:
  where readers stop most. Helps writers find slow chapters; M; fits WRITE-2.
- **Library export.** Include the reader's shelves in "Download my data" (it is personal data anyway). S; fits
  PRIVACY.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible as a fourth reading font.** Self-hosted (OFL licence, no third-party script), built for
  low-vision readers. The board already names it for the apps; it can work in the browser now. S; fits READ-4 or later.
- **Night theme by the clock.** An option to switch the chapter to Night after a chosen hour (for example 21:00),
  using only the device's clock. Kinder for bedtime readers, many of them teens; S.
- **Reading ruler.** A soft band that highlights the current line, toggled in Reading settings. Helps readers with
  dyslexia or ADHD keep their place; M.
- **Settings preview sentence.** A short sample paragraph at the top of the panel, so readers on a long chapter
  see the change without closing the panel; S.
- **"Most shared this week" shelf.** A Discover shelf ordered by the new share totals (aggregates only, never
  who shared). Gives readers a different door into stories than likes; S; fits DISCOVER.
- **Quote card in the writer's own colours.** The writer picks which of our palette colours their story's cards
  default to, so a story's cards look like a set on social media; S; fits READ-4 or later.
- **Share a whole story, not just a chapter.** "Share" on the story page with the same clean link and chat
  preview, pointing at chapter 1's teaser; S.
- **"Most shared passages" for writers.** On the writer dashboard, the paragraphs readers link to most (counted per paragraph per day, never who), so a writer sees which lines land. Why: writers on Substack love seeing their most-highlighted lines; it rewards craft without any personal data. (Seen while building S1-E4-11.)
- **Picture links in the preview image.** Once a passed picture can have a safe public copy, a picture link's preview shows the picture itself, not the cover colour. Why: picture links would stand out in chats, which is the whole point of a picture card. (S1-E4-11 follow-up.)
